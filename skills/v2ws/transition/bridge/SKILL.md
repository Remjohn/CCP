# 🌉 TRANSITION-01: BRIDGE TO SELLING

> **Mode:** TENSION → RECOGNITION | **Agent:** Alessandro | **Duration:** 3-5 minutes

## Purpose
Smoothly transition from content delivery to the close sequence. This is the most dangerous moment in a webinar — if it feels jarring, trust collapses.

## Bridge Framework
1. **Recap the value:** "So we've covered X, Y, and Z..."
2. **Plant the gap:** "But there's one thing I couldn't cover in this session..."
3. **Name the limitation:** "The truth is, knowing WHAT to do isn't enough..."
4. **Open the door:** "Which is why I want to share something else with you..."

## Generation Rules
1. Never use "Now let me sell you something" energy
2. The bridge must feel like a natural continuation of teaching
3. Reference specific content steps from earlier
4. Create curiosity about what comes next (not pressure)
5. Max 150 words
