# 🚀 TRANSITION-02: MOMENTUM BUILDER

> **Mode:** RECOGNITION | **Agent:** Alessandro | **Duration:** 3-5 minutes

## Purpose
Stack evidence of transformation rapidly to create a sense of inevitability — "this works, and it can work for me too."

## Momentum Stack
1. **Quick wins compilation:** 3-5 rapid testimonials or results
2. **Pattern recognition:** "Notice the pattern? Every one of them started exactly where you are."
3. **Identity bridge:** "They're not different from you. They just had the right framework."

## Generation Rules
1. Use ≥3 different proof types (testimonial, data, before/after)
2. Speed increases — shorter segments, faster cadence
3. Each proof must connect back to one of the content steps taught
4. End with the audience feeling "I could be next"
5. Max 200 words
