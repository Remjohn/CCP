# 📋 TRANSITION-03: RECAP REINFORCER

> **Mode:** RECOGNITION | **Agent:** Alessandro | **Duration:** 2-3 minutes

## Purpose
Crystallize everything taught into 3-5 memorable takeaways that the audience can carry forward — and that naturally set up the close.

## Recap Structure
1. **"Here's what we covered:"** Bullet-point summary (3-5 items)
2. **"The one thing to remember:"** Single most important insight
3. **"But here's the challenge:"** Acknowledge implementation difficulty
4. **"Which is exactly why..."** → Bridge to close

## Generation Rules
1. Maximum 5 recap points
2. Each point in ≤10 words
3. Use the coach's metaphor system (from coach_soul.json)
4. The "challenge" acknowledgment must feel empathetic, not pressuring
5. Max 150 words
