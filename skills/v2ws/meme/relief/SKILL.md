# 😅 RELIEF THEORY

**Principle:** Humor functions as a pressure valve — releasing built-up tension or anxiety.

## Application in Webinars
- Deploy AFTER intense teaching sections to release cognitive load
- After a heavy truth bomb, follow with a self-deprecating joke
- Example: After a challenging content section: "If you're still with me after that, you deserve a medal. Or at least a coffee."

## Formula: `[Build tension through teaching] + [Release through humor] = Relief`
