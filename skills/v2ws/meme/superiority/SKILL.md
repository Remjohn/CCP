# 😎 SUPERIORITY THEORY

**Principle:** Humor arises from feeling superior to a past state, a common mistake, or an outdated belief.

## Application in Webinars
- Help audience laugh at their PAST selves (not current selves — that's mean)
- "Remember when you thought X was a good idea? Yeah, we all did."
- Creates an in-group feeling — "we're all smarter now"

## Formula: `[Something we used to believe] + [Why it's obviously wrong now] = Humor`
