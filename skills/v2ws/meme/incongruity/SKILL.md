# 🤔 INCONGRUITY THEORY

**Principle:** Humor arises from the unexpected — when reality contradicts expectation.

## Application in Webinars
- Juxtapose what the audience THINKS they should do vs. what actually works
- Example: Slide showing "Expert advice" (complex diagram) vs. "What actually works" (simple sticky note)

## Formula: `[What you expect] vs. [What's actually true] = Humor`
