# 🔄 CONTENT-02: STEP TRANSFORMATION

> **Mode:** VULNERABILITY | **Agent:** Elene | **Duration:** 8-12 min per step

## Purpose
Teach each step with enough depth that the audience can take action, but not so much that they feel overwhelmed. The balance: enough to believe, not enough to not need you.

## Teaching Framework per Step
1. **WHAT:** Name the step clearly ("Step 2: The Mirror Method")
2. **WHY:** Why this step matters more than they think
3. **HOW:** The specific actions (simplified — not full training)
4. **PROOF:** Quick win example or case study
5. **TRANSITION:** Link to next step ("Now that you have X, the natural next question is...")

## Generation Rules
1. Each step follows the WHAT→WHY→HOW→PROOF→TRANSITION arc
2. HOW section gives 80/20 — enough to take first action, not full methodology
3. Include a "common mistake" per step (builds credibility)
4. Slides: max 15 slides per step
