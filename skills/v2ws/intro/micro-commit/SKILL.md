# ✅ INTRO-005: MICRO-COMMITMENT BUILDER

> **Mode:** RECOGNITION | **Agent:** Alessandro | **Duration:** 1-2 minutes

## Purpose
Build progressive compliance through small, easy "yes" responses. Each micro-commitment makes the final ask (CTA) feel like a natural continuation rather than a sudden demand.

## Micro-Commitment Sequence
1. **Identity confirmation:** "If you're someone who [core desire], type 'yes' in the chat."
2. **Pain acknowledgment:** "If you've ever felt [core fear], you're not alone."
3. **Commitment to learn:** "Are you willing to stay open-minded for the next 45 minutes?"
4. **Action preview:** "If what I share makes sense, would you at least consider trying it?"

## Generation Rules
1. Start with the easiest "yes" and escalate gradually
2. Use tribe language (from tribe_soul.json)
3. Each micro-commitment must feel genuine, not manipulative
4. Connect at least one commitment to a core desire
5. Max 100 words
