# 🛡️ INTRO-006: INTRO OBJECTIONS

> **Mode:** TENSION | **Agent:** Alessandro | **Duration:** 2-3 minutes

## Purpose
Pre-handle the audience's top objections before they crystallize into resistance. This is inoculation — name the objection, validate it, dissolve it.

## Objection Handling Framework
1. **Name it:** "You might be thinking [objection]..."
2. **Validate it:** "And honestly? That's fair. I thought the same thing."
3. **Reframe it:** "But here's what I discovered..."
4. **Redirect:** "So let me ask you to hold that thought and give me [X minutes]."

## Common Objection Categories
- **Time:** "I don't have time for this"
- **Complexity:** "This won't work for my situation"
- **Skepticism:** "I've heard this before"
- **Cost:** "I can't afford this" (handled lightly in intro, fully in close)
- **Identity:** "This isn't for someone like me"

## Generation Rules
1. Address 3-5 objections maximum
2. Pull from tribe_soul.json (suspicions, hidden_beliefs)
3. Use GhostContext to surface objections the audience won't voice
4. Never be defensive — always empathetic then reframing
5. Max 250 words
