# 🏛️ INTRO-002: POSITIONING & AUTHORITY

> **Mode:** TENSION → RECOGNITION | **Agent:** Alessandro | **Duration:** 3-5 minutes

## Purpose
Establish the coach as the legitimate guide WITHOUT resorting to ego-driven bragging. Authority is demonstrated through transformation proof, not credentials.

## Positioning Framework

1. **The Before:** "I was exactly where you are..." (vulnerability)
2. **The Discovery:** "Until I discovered..." (unique mechanism reveal)
3. **The Proof:** "And here's what happened..." (transformation evidence)
4. **The Bridge:** "Which is exactly what I'm going to show you today..." (promise connection)

## Generation Rules
1. Lead with vulnerability, not achievements
2. The unique mechanism (from coach_soul.json) must be named explicitly
3. Include ≥1 specific, verifiable transformation result
4. Transition naturally to the promise ("By the end of this session, you'll...")
5. No resume/CV recitation — proof through results only
