# 🔮 INTRO-004: INTRIGUE CREATION

> **Mode:** TENSION | **Agent:** Alessandro | **Duration:** 2-3 minutes

## Purpose
Create open loops that make the audience feel they CANNOT leave without closing them. Intrigue is the mechanism that keeps attention locked.

## Open Loop Patterns
1. **The Teaser:** "In about 20 minutes, I'm going to reveal the one thing that changed everything for me — and it's not what you think."
2. **The Counter-intuitive:** "I'm going to show you why the thing most experts recommend is actually making your problem worse."
3. **The Exclusive:** "What I'm about to share took me 3 years and $100K to figure out. You're getting it in the next 45 minutes."

## Generation Rules
1. Create ≥3 open loops (one per major content section)
2. Each loop must reference a specific content section
3. Use tribe emotional triggers (from tribe_soul.json)
4. Never reveal the answer in the intrigue — only the promise of an answer
5. Max 150 words
