# 🔄 CLOSE-02: OLD HABITS CLOSE

> **Mode:** TENSION | **Agent:** Alessandro | **Duration:** 2-3 minutes

## Purpose
Name the enemy: old habits. The audience already knows what's wrong — the challenge is breaking free from comfortable patterns.

## Framework
1. **Name the pattern:** "Here's what usually happens after a webinar like this..."
2. **Predict the failure:** "You'll feel inspired for 24 hours. Then life takes over."
3. **Identify the mechanism:** "Your old habits have gravity — they pull you back."
4. **Break the loop:** "The only way to break a pattern is to make a NEW decision right now."

## Generation Rules
1. Be honest about post-webinar letdown (builds credibility)
2. Use the coach's confrontational register (from ttt_baseline)
3. Don't guilt — illuminate
4. Max 150 words
