# ⏳ CLOSE-04: DO NOTHING CLOSE

> **Mode:** TENSION | **Agent:** Alessandro | **Duration:** 2-3 minutes

## Purpose
Make the "do nothing" option feel like an active CHOICE with real consequences, not a passive default.

## Framework
1. **Legitimize the option:** "You absolutely can do nothing. That's a valid choice."
2. **Project the outcome:** "Here's what 'nothing' looks like in 12 months..."
3. **Remove the comfort:** "Doing nothing isn't free — you're paying for it with [time/opportunity]."
4. **Reframe:** "So the real question isn't 'can I afford this?' — it's 'can I afford not to?'"

## Generation Rules
1. Never shame the audience for considering inaction
2. Use specific, concrete projections (not vague doom)
3. Position doing nothing as a DECISION, not an absence of decision
4. Max 150 words
