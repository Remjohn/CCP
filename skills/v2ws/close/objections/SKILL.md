# 🛡️ CLOSE-06: CLOSE OBJECTIONS

> **Mode:** RECOGNITION | **Agent:** Alessandro | **Duration:** 5-8 minutes

## Purpose
Handle the final objections that stand between desire and action. These are different from intro objections — they're buying objections.

## Objection Framework (LAPS)
- **L — Listen:** "I hear you saying..."
- **A — Acknowledge:** "And that's completely fair..."
- **P — Pivot:** "But let me ask you this..."
- **S — Solve:** "Here's how we handle that..."

## Common Close Objections
| Objection | Reframe |
|-----------|---------|
| "I can't afford it" | Cost vs. investment. What's the cost of NOT doing this? |
| "I need to think about it" | What specifically do you need to think about? |
| "I need to ask my spouse" | Bring them next time, or: what would they say about your goals? |
| "Now's not the right time" | When IS the right time? And what changes between now and then? |
| "I've tried similar things" | What was different? Our approach is X (unique mechanism). |

## Generation Rules
1. Handle 4-6 objections maximum
2. Use GhostContext to surface unvoiced objections
3. Every reframe must reference content taught earlier
4. Never be defensive or pushy
5. End with a final, soft CTA
