# 💊 CLOSE-03: PAIN RELIEF CLOSE

> **Mode:** TENSION | **Agent:** Alessandro | **Duration:** 2-3 minutes

## Purpose
Make the cost of inaction viscerally real. Not through fear-mongering, but through honest projection of where they'll be in 6/12 months if nothing changes.

## Framework
1. **Project forward:** "Imagine it's 6 months from now and nothing has changed..."
2. **Feel the weight:** "Same frustrations. Same results. Same conversations with yourself."
3. **Name the cost:** "That's not just time lost — it's [specific cost: money, relationships, health]."
4. **Offer relief:** "Or... you could decide today that enough is enough."

## Generation Rules
1. Use tribe-specific pain points (from tribe_soul.json core_fears)
2. Be specific — generic pain doesn't land
3. The relief comes from DECISION, not from buying
4. Max 150 words
