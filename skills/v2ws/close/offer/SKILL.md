# 💎 CLOSE-05: OFFER

> **Mode:** RECOGNITION | **Agent:** Alessandro | **Duration:** 5-8 minutes

## Purpose
Present the offer with absolute clarity. No confusion about what they get, what it costs, and what their next step is.

## Offer Structure
1. **Name it:** "Introducing [Program Name]..."
2. **What's included:** Bullet-point list of deliverables (modules, calls, resources)
3. **Value stack:** Assign value to each component
4. **Price reveal:** "Your investment is..."
5. **Guarantee:** Risk reversal (money-back, results guarantee, etc.)
6. **CTA:** "Here's exactly what to do right now..."

## Generation Rules
1. Every deliverable must connect to a pain point or desire addressed in content
2. Value stacking should feel genuine, not inflated
3. The guarantee must be specific (not vague "satisfaction guaranteed")
4. CTA is ONE clear action (click, call, apply)
5. Use coach's voice for the entire offer presentation
