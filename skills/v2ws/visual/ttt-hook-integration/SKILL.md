# 🌡️ V²WS TTT × VISUAL HOOK INTEGRATION

Maps the TTT temperature system to visual design parameters. Low-TTT content gets clean, minimal visuals. High-TTT content gets bold, confrontational visuals.

## TTT → Visual Mapping

| TTT Range | Color Palette | Typography | Visual Density | Animation |
|-----------|---------------|------------|----------------|-----------|
| TTT-01 to TTT-03 | Muted, pastel | Serif, elegant | Low — white space | Gentle fade |
| TTT-04 to TTT-06 | Balanced, warm | Sans-serif, clear | Medium — structured | Smooth slide |
| TTT-07 to TTT-09 | Bold, contrast | Impact, heavy | High — packed | Hard cut |
