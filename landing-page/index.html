What We Built

A multi-agent platform that replaces an entire production team

Conscious Elite is a multi-agent SaaS platform that automates premium content creation, client relationship intelligence, and conversion systems for professional coaches. The platform uses proprietary Voice DNA extraction, psychologically-routed content generation, and a zero-friction delivery system via Telegram to enable coaches to maintain deep 1:1 relationships with hundreds of clients simultaneously.

The system comprises 6 interconnected AI sub-systems, 76 named agents across 6 operational departments, 180+ skills and tools, and supports registered coaches in our active pilot phase. The architecture was developed over 3 years of rigorous iteration with production coaching businesses.

How We Use AI Infrastructure

Our platform leverages large language models for multi-step agentic reasoning, speech-to-text transcription (Whisper), text-to-image generation for visual content pipelines, and real-time sentiment analysis for client behavioral tracking.

We currently utilize Groq, NVIDIA NIM endpoints, and OpenRouter for inference routing, with plans to scale to dedicated GPU instances as our user base grows past the pilot phase into full production.
