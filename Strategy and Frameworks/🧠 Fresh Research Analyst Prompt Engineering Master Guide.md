# <a id="_7p01uk9djrpz"></a>__🧠 Fresh Research Analyst Prompt Engineering Master Guide__

## <a id="_x23cbqo2kkcw"></a>__The Strategic Framework for Creating Specialized Intelligence Agents__

## <a id="_6om0cx1a3awe"></a>__🧠 FOUNDATIONAL PHILOSOPHY__

Each Fresh Research Analyst is a __hyper\-specialized intelligence extraction engine__ designed to serve one specific creative archetype\. They are not general researchers—they are tactical specialists who understand:

1. __The Emotional Core__ of their assigned archetype
2. __The Specific Intelligence__ their creative counterpart needs
3. __The Voice Alignment__ required for seamless integration
4. __The Quality Standards__ that make content undeniable

## <a id="_phaw7bbxlyqg"></a>__📋 THE 7\-STEP PROMPT ENGINEERING PROTOCOL__

### <a id="_s3bi53nxkkdo"></a>__STEP 1: ARCHETYPE ANALYSIS & ROLE DEFINITION__

__Objective:__ Define the analyst's specialized hunting focus

__Method:__

1\. Identify the target archetype \(e\.g\., "The Achievement Story"\)

2\. Analyze its emotional purpose \(e\.g\., inspiration, motivation, proof\)

3\. Define its narrative strategy \(e\.g\., hero's journey, transformation arc\)

4\. Determine its audience effect \(e\.g\., "I can do this too\!"\)

__Template for Role Definition:__

ROLE: You are "The \[EMOTION\] Hunter"

\- Expert in identifying \[SPECIFIC DATA TYPE\] from real\-time feeds

\- Specialist in \[NARRATIVE STRATEGY\] evidence gathering

\- Mission: Find intelligence that creates \[AUDIENCE EFFECT\]

__Example Output:__

ROLE: You are "The Achievement Hunter"

\- Expert in identifying success stories, milestone data, and transformation metrics

\- Specialist in hero's journey evidence gathering  

\- Mission: Find intelligence that makes the audience think "If they can do it, so can I"

### <a id="_vhlzqwe5avck"></a>__STEP 2: INTELLIGENCE TAXONOMY MAPPING__

__Objective:__ Define exactly what types of intelligence this analyst hunts for

__Method:__

1\. Primary Intelligence Type \(the "main dish"\)

2\. Supporting Intelligence Types \(2\-3 "side dishes"\)

3\. Humanization Element \(the "emotional bridge"\)

4\. Verification Requirements \(the "credibility filter"\)

__Framework Template:__

INTELLIGENCE TARGETS:

A\. PRIMARY: \[Specific data type that serves the archetype's core purpose\]

B\. SUPPORTING: \[2\-3 additional data types that reinforce the primary\]

C\. HUMANIZATION: \[Personal story/anecdote type that makes it relatable\]

D\. VERIFICATION: \[Source and recency requirements\]

__Example for "Achievement Story":__

INTELLIGENCE TARGETS:

A\. PRIMARY: Recent success stories with specific metrics \(revenue, time, transformation\)

B\. SUPPORTING: Industry growth data, expert validation quotes, methodology details

C\. HUMANIZATION: Personal background, obstacles overcome, specific timeline

D\. VERIFICATION: Named individuals, company verification, last 6 months maximum

### <a id="_eld6qazg5vpv"></a>__STEP 3: EMOTIONAL TEMPERATURE CALIBRATION__

__Objective:__ Define how this analyst should "read" and "write" emotional intelligence

__Method:__

1\. Identify the archetype's emotional spectrum \(e\.g\., hope → excitement → determination\)

2\. Define the "search bias" \(what emotional angles to prioritize\)

3\. Set the "voice temperature" \(how to present findings\)

4\. Establish "resonance filters" \(what makes intelligence emotionally sticky\)

__Template:__

EMOTIONAL CALIBRATION:

\- Search Priority: Look for intelligence that evokes \[PRIMARY EMOTION\]

\- Voice Temperature: Present findings with \[TONE DESCRIPTOR\] energy

\- Resonance Filter: Prioritize stories that create \[SPECIFIC FEELING\]

\- Avoid: Intelligence that triggers \[CONFLICTING EMOTIONS\]

### <a id="_7aotnieycq0"></a>__STEP 4: ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK DESIGN__

__Objective:__ Create the structured extraction methodology

__Method:__

1\. Define 3\-4 analysis sections \(usually 400\-600 words total\)

2\. Assign word counts and purposes to each section

3\. Create quality criteria for each section

4\. Establish section interconnection logic

__Universal Framework Template:__

ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK \(Target: \[X\]\-\[Y\] words total\):

A\. THE \[SECTION 1 NAME\] \(\[X\]\-\[Y\] words\)

   Purpose: \[What this section accomplishes\]

   What to Look For: \[Specific intelligence types\]

   Quality Criteria: \[Standards for inclusion\]

B\. THE \[SECTION 2 NAME\] \(\[X\]\-\[Y\] words\)

   Purpose: \[What this section accomplishes\]

   What to Look For: \[Specific intelligence types\]

   Quality Criteria: \[Standards for inclusion\]

\[Continue pattern for remaining sections\]

### <a id="_d08uzb45t7q8"></a>__STEP 5: VOICE ALIGNMENT PROTOCOL INTEGRATION__

__Objective:__ Ensure every analyst writes in the client's authentic voice

__Standard Integration \(Use This Exact Block\):__

TONE EMULATION PROTOCOL:

SOUL ANALYSIS PHASE:

\- Analyze the client's \{Conscious\_Soul\_Values\}

\- Identify their "internal temperature" on the specific content topic

\- Extract their unique metaphors and vocabulary for \[ARCHETYPE\-SPECIFIC COMMUNICATION\]

\- Determine their communication style for \[ARCHETYPE\-SPECIFIC DELIVERY\]

VOICE EMBODIMENT PHASE:

\- Write as if the client personally \[ARCHETYPE\-SPECIFIC ACTION\]

\- Use their signature phrases and metaphors to frame the \[INTELLIGENCE TYPE\]

\- Match their emotional intensity level and \[ARCHETYPE\-SPECIFIC STYLE\]

__Customization Examples:__

- __Achievement Story:__ "communication style for celebrating success"
- __Cautionary Tale:__ "communication style for delivering warnings"
- __Discovery Story:__ "communication style for sharing revelations"

### <a id="_z63qe1bqp06a"></a>__STEP 6: OUTPUT STRUCTURE SPECIFICATION__

__Objective:__ Define the exact deliverable format and requirements

__Method:__

1\. Specify total word count range

2\. Define required sections with clear names

3\. Set opening and closing requirements

4\. Establish formatting standards

__Universal Template:__

OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS:

Format: A concise, structured text document \(\[X\]\-\[Y\] words\) with clear, client\-voiced sections\.

Required Sections:

1\. "\[OPENING SECTION NAME\]" \(1\-2 sentences setting \[SPECIFIC TONE\]\)

2\. "\[MAIN SECTION 1 NAME\]" \(The \[Purpose\]\)

3\. "\[MAIN SECTION 2 NAME\]" \(The \[Purpose\]\)

4\. "\[MAIN SECTION 3 NAME\]" \(The \[Purpose\]\)

5\. "\[CLOSING SECTION NAME\]" \(1\-2 sentences that \[Specific Function\]\)

CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS:

\- \[Archetype\-specific success criterion 1\]

\- \[Archetype\-specific success criterion 2\]

\- \[Archetype\-specific success criterion 3\]

\- \[Universal voice consistency requirement\]

### <a id="_bd1cn8bffvl"></a>__STEP 7: QUALITY ASSURANCE CRITERIA__

__Objective:__ Build in automated quality control

__Method:__

1\. Define archetype\-specific assessment questions

2\. Set minimum standards for each criterion

3\. Create failure mode prevention

4\. Establish system integration checks

__Assessment Template:__

\[ARCHETYPE\] ASSESSMENT CRITERIA:

For each piece of intelligence, ask:

\- Relevance: Does this directly serve the \[ARCHETYPE PURPOSE\]?

\- "\[ARCHETYPE\-SPECIFIC QUALITY\]": \[Specific quality question\]?

\- Verifiability: Is the source clear and credible?

\- Client Alignment: Does this align with the client's core values?

\- Emotional Impact: Will this create the desired \[EMOTIONAL RESPONSE\]?

## <a id="_k55b3y8jhdvt"></a>__🎭 ARCHETYPE\-SPECIFIC CUSTOMIZATION EXAMPLES__

### <a id="_2dbyf5g9w326"></a>__Example 1: The Achievement Story Fresh Research Analyst__

__Step 1 \- Role Definition:__

ROLE: You are "The Achievement Hunter"

Expert in identifying success stories, milestone achievements, and transformation metrics from real\-time feeds that prove extraordinary results are possible for ordinary people\.

__Step 2 \- Intelligence Taxonomy:__

INTELLIGENCE TARGETS:

A\. PRIMARY: Recent success stories with specific, verifiable metrics \(revenue growth, time saved, health improvements, etc\.\)

B\. SUPPORTING: Industry benchmarks, expert validation, methodology details that contextualize the achievement

C\. HUMANIZATION: Personal background, specific obstacles overcome, relatable starting point

D\. VERIFICATION: Named individuals or companies, verifiable outcomes, timeline within last 6 months

__Step 4 \- Analytical Framework:__

A\. THE BREAKTHROUGH MOMENT \(150\-200 words\)

   Purpose: Identify the single most inspiring recent achievement

   What to Look For: Specific person \+ specific metric \+ specific timeframe

   Quality Criteria: Must be verifiable, recent, and create "holy sh\*t" reaction

B\. THE METHOD BEHIND THE MAGIC \(200\-250 words\)

   Purpose: Extract the strategy/system that created the achievement

   What to Look For: Specific tactics, tools, or approaches mentioned

   Quality Criteria: Must be actionable and replicable by the audience

C\. THE HUMAN JOURNEY \(150\-200 words\)

   Purpose: Find the relatable struggle that makes success feel attainable

   What to Look For: Starting point, obstacles, mindset shifts, timeline

   Quality Criteria: Must create identification and remove "they're just lucky" thinking

### <a id="_dxr6idrqfjw"></a>__Example 2: The Fear\-Anxiety Listicle Fresh Research Analyst__

__Step 1 \- Role Definition:__

ROLE: You are "The Threat Intelligence Hunter"

Expert in identifying emerging dangers, warning statistics, and cautionary data from real\-time feeds that help people protect themselves from genuine risks\.

__Step 2 \- Intelligence Taxonomy:__

INTELLIGENCE TARGETS:

A\. PRIMARY: Recent alarming statistics or emerging threats with clear, credible sourcing

B\. SUPPORTING: Expert warnings, trend data, protective recommendations from authorities

C\. HUMANIZATION: Real stories of people affected by these threats, with lessons learned

D\. VERIFICATION: Government sources, academic studies, reputable news organizations, last 3 months

## <a id="_7lj17v9vc117"></a>__🔧 IMPLEMENTATION WORKFLOW__

### <a id="_2basilccdicm"></a>__Phase 1: Batch Creation \(1\-2 hours per archetype\)__

1. Run through all 7 steps for each archetype
2. Create first draft prompt using the templates
3. Test with sample raw\_api\_output data
4. Refine based on output quality

### <a id="_wtw9lay5vbk5"></a>__Phase 2: Quality Testing \(30 minutes per prompt\)__

1. Test with 3 different raw search outputs
2. Evaluate voice consistency across outputs
3. Check adherence to word count requirements
4. Verify intelligence quality meets standards

### <a id="_xqi44bwfp9oa"></a>__Phase 3: System Integration \(15 minutes per prompt\)__

1. Format for Supabase storage
2. Assign unique prompt\_id
3. Add to fresh\_research\_analyst\_protocols table
4. Test n8n workflow integration

### <a id="_mwjztq1fxol0"></a>__Phase 4: Continuous Optimization__

1. Monitor output quality weekly
2. Collect creative agent feedback
3. Refine prompts based on performance data
4. Update taxonomy as new intelligence patterns emerge

## <a id="_j1z61dvtuivg"></a>__🎯 QUALITY BENCHMARKS__

### <a id="_yh5p9kbx9qw5"></a>__Universal Standards \(Apply to All Analysts\):__

- ✅ Maintains client voice consistency \(95%\+ recognizable as their style\)
- ✅ Meets word count requirements \(within ±10% of target\)
- ✅ Provides verifiable sources for all claims
- ✅ Delivers tactically useful intelligence \(directly supports creative script\)

### <a id="_iyx36k38k7py"></a>__Archetype\-Specific Standards:__

- ✅ Creates the intended emotional response in test audiences
- ✅ Provides intelligence that enhances rather than competes with the deep research
- ✅ Identifies "pattern interrupt" elements that feel current and urgent
- ✅ Supports the archetype's narrative strategy with relevant data

Establish credibility hierarchy:

MID TIER: Reputable news organizations, industry associations, verified experts

LOW TIER: Blogs, social media, unverified sources \(use sparingly\)
