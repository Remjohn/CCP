### <a id="_s5pallqg9m99"></a>__How the New 22 Agent Modules Will Operate__

This is the core of your question\. Since you are retiring the two monolithic idea generation prompts, here is the new, more sophisticated process for the "Content Idea Generation" workflow, as envisioned by your manifesto\.

Think of it as having one __Orchestrator Agent__ that manages a team of 22 __Specialist Agents__\.

__Step 1: Foundation Analysis \(The Orchestrator Starts\)__ The workflow is triggered by a new Context\_Premise\. The Orchestrator Agent analyzes this premise, identifying the core DHDs, fears, dreams, and emotional triggers\. It also activates the "Cultural Intelligence Network" to get a snapshot of the current cultural moment relevant to the premise\.

__Step 2: Framework Combination \(The Orchestrator Strategizes\)__ The Orchestrator consults its __Combination Chemistry__ rules \(the Synergy Matrix you will define\)\. It decides on a potent fusion of frameworks\. For example, it might decide the best strategy is a __Triple\-Layer Fusion__:

- __Base Layer \(Value\):__ Practical Value
- __Enhancement Layer \(Emotion\):__ Fear/Insecurity Angle
- __Amplification Layer \(Emotion\):__ Relief Peak

__Step 3: Activating the Specialist Agents__ The Orchestrator now calls upon the specific agent modules it has selected\. In this case, it activates three specialists:

- The Practical Value Agent
- The Fear/Insecurity Angle Agent
- The Relief Peak Agent

__Step 4: Idea Component Generation \(The Specialists Work\)__ Each specialist agent, using its own unique research angles and SPR triggers, generates *components* of an idea based on the Context\_Premise\.

- __Practical Value Agent__: Generates actionable advice components \(e\.g\., "a 5\-minute daily habit that builds wealth"\)\.
- __Fear/Insecurity Agent__: Generates threat\-amplification components \(e\.g\., "the financial mistakes your friends are making that will keep them broke forever"\)\.
- __Relief Peak Agent__: Generates solution\-reveal components \(e\.g\., "the simple escape route from financial anxiety"\)\.

__Step 5: Fusion and Title Creation \(The Orchestrator Composes\)__ The Orchestrator takes the components from the specialist agents and, using its __Fusion Protocols__, combines them into a single, powerful, and complete viral content idea with a full title\.

- __Resulting Title:__ *"The Wealth\-Building Method Your Parents Never Taught You \(While Your Friends Stay Broke Forever\) \- Plus the 5\-Minute Daily Habit That Changes Everything"*\.

__Step 6: Quality Scoring and Validation__ The Orchestrator scores this new idea against the __Viral Potential Formula__ from the manifesto\. It checks for high Emotional Resonance, a strong Surprise Factor, and low Cognitive Load\.

__Step 7: Iteration__ The Orchestrator repeats this process, trying different framework combinations \(e\.g\., maybe next it tries "Timeline Evolution" \+ "Dopamine Cliff"\) until it has generated the required 12 high\-scoring viral content ideas\. These 12 ideas are then saved to your Supabase table, ready for the next workflow\.
