# <a id="_pckeuyksvvnt"></a>__🎯 Deep Research Analyst Prompt Engineering Master Guide__

## <a id="_o4rrxxxusnpj"></a>__Overview__

This systematic guide will help you create high\-quality, specialized Deep Research Analyst prompts for each of your content archetypes\. Each analyst must be a domain expert who can extract the most strategically relevant intelligence from your 30\+ page research documents and deliver it in the client's authentic voice\.

## <a id="_ausd64vh1xu4"></a>__PHASE 1: ARCHETYPE ANALYSIS & ROLE DEFINITION__

### <a id="_ydvt28swbsi8"></a>__Step 1: Decode the Creative Archetype__

Before writing a single word, deeply understand the target archetype:

__Questions to Answer:__

- What is the core emotional purpose of this archetype?
- What specific type of content does it create?
- What emotional journey does it take the audience on?
- What makes this archetype unique from others in its category?

__Example Analysis:__

- __The Achievement Story__: Creates narrative content → Emotional journey from struggle to triumph → Unique focus on personal transformation
- __The Fear\-Anxiety Listicle__: Creates list\-based content → Emotional journey from ignorance to awareness → Unique focus on protective knowledge

### <a id="_9msasj1vx7i"></a>__Step 2: Define the Analyst's Specialized Role__

Create a compelling "role identity" that matches the archetype's needs:

__Role Naming Formula:__ "The \[Adjective\] \[Metaphor\]" \+ Brief Role Description

__Examples:__

- __Achievement Story__ → "The Victory Archaeologist" \(excavates triumph narratives\)
- __Fear\-Anxiety Listicle__ → "The Threat Intelligence Officer" \(identifies protective knowledge\)
- __Curiosity Story__ → "The Mystery Detective" \(uncovers fascinating unknowns\)
- __Nostalgia Content__ → "The Time Capsule Curator" \(preserves meaningful memories\)

### <a id="_heh3jy8gf96g"></a>__Step 3: Define What Intelligence This Archetype Needs__

Each archetype requires different types of research intelligence\. Map these systematically:

__Intelligence Categories Framework:__

__Category__

__Purpose__

__Best For Archetypes__

__Emotional Triggers__

All storytelling, humor, inspirational

__Contrarian Evidence__

Information that challenges mainstream beliefs

__Historical Patterns__

__Human Impact Stories__

__Statistical Ammunition__

__Expert Insights__

__Cultural Context__

### <a id="_8f6ocm7z76ix"></a>__Step 4: Create the Analytical Framework__

Design 3\-5 specific research extraction categories for your archetype, with substantial content expectations:

__Framework Template:__

A\. \[PRIMARY INTELLIGENCE TYPE\] \(400\-500 words\)

   \- What specifically to look for

   \- Quality criteria for depth and detail

   \- Usage purpose and creative application

   \- Expected narrative development

B\. \[SECONDARY INTELLIGENCE TYPE\] \(300\-400 words\)

   \- What specifically to look for

   \- Quality criteria for depth and detail

   \- Usage purpose and creative application

   \- Expected narrative development

\[Continue for 3\-5 categories totaling 1600\-1800 words\]

__Example \- The Achievement Story Framework:__

A\. TRANSFORMATIONAL MOMENTS \(500 words\)

   \- Detailed before/after scenarios from the research with full context

   \- Must show dramatic personal change with supporting evidence

   \- Purpose: Create rich narrative arc of triumph with believable progression

   \- Should include: background context, turning point details, outcome specifics

B\. STRUGGLE UNIVERSALITY \(400 words\)

   \- Comprehensive analysis of common challenges with statistical backing

   \- Must be relatable to target audience with emotional resonance

   \- Purpose: Build deep audience connection through shared experience

   \- Should include: prevalence data, emotional impact, cultural context

C\. SUCCESS STRATEGIES \(400 words\)

   \- Detailed tactics and mindsets with implementation guidance

   \- Must be actionable and replicable with evidence of effectiveness

   \- Purpose: Provide comprehensive "how" of achievement

   \- Should include: step\-by\-step processes, success metrics, expert validation

D\. INSPIRATIONAL EVIDENCE \(300 words\)

   \- Supporting research that reinforces possibility of transformation

   \- Scientific studies, expert testimonials, cultural proof points

   \- Purpose: Build credibility and hope for audience

   \- Should include: research citations, expert credentials, outcome data

## <a id="_5pi0lzf8umq6"></a>__PHASE 3: VOICE INTEGRATION PROTOCOL__

### <a id="_1pgik3izplwa"></a>__Step 5: Design the Tone Emulation Process__

Every analyst must write in the client's authentic voice\. Create specific instructions:

__Standard Tone Emulation Protocol:__

1\. SOUL ANALYSIS PHASE:

   \- Analyze client's \{Conscious\_Soul\_Values\}

   \- Identify their "internal temperature" on \[ARCHETYPE TOPIC\]

   \- Extract their unique metaphors and vocabulary

   \- Determine their communication style preferences

2\. VOICE EMBODIMENT PHASE:

   \- Write as if the client discovered this research personally

   \- Use their signature phrases and metaphors

   \- Match their emotional intensity level

   \- Adopt their typical sentence structures

3\. AUTHENTICITY CHECK:

   \- Would this sound natural coming from the client?

   \- Does it match their worldview and values?

   \- Are their unique perspectives woven throughout?

### <a id="_ysiq4lcx2t3v"></a>__Step 6: Customize Voice Integration for Your Archetype__

Add archetype\-specific voice considerations:

__Questions to Address:__

- How does this client typically discuss \[ARCHETYPE TOPIC\]?
- What metaphors would they use for this type of content?
- What's their comfort level with \[EMOTIONAL TONE\] content?
- How do they typically structure information for this format?

## <a id="_qnzb0wo2gj87"></a>__PHASE 4: QUALITY STANDARDS & ASSESSMENT__

### <a id="_rtszdqcz63f3"></a>__Step 7: Define Quality Criteria__

Create specific standards for evaluating the intelligence brief:

__Universal Quality Standards:__

- __Relevance__: Does each piece directly serve the archetype's purpose?
- __Impact__: Would this information significantly enhance the final content?
- __Authenticity__: Does it sound like it came from the client?
- __Actionability__: Can the creative agent immediately use this intelligence?
- __Verifiability__: Is it traceable back to the source research?
- __Comprehensiveness__: Does it fully leverage the 30\+ page research investment?
- __Narrative Depth__: Is each section developed with sufficient detail and context?

__Archetype\-Specific Standards:__ Add 2\-3 quality criteria unique to your archetype:

__Examples:__

- __Achievement Story__: "Inspiration Quotient" \- Does this make people believe change is possible?
- __Shocking Listicle__: "Jaw\-Drop Factor" \- Would this make someone immediately want to share it?
- __Fear\-Anxiety Content__: "Protection Value" \- Does this help people avoid real danger?

### <a id="_hz5gm3ifuk1h"></a>__Step 8: Create Assessment Questions__

Design specific questions to evaluate each piece of intelligence:

__Template:__

For each extracted piece of intelligence, ask:

1\. \[RELEVANCE QUESTION specific to archetype\]

2\. \[IMPACT QUESTION specific to emotional goal\]

3\. \[AUTHENTICITY QUESTION about voice match\]

4\. \[QUALITY QUESTION specific to archetype standards\]

## <a id="_fsnf7zlch47l"></a>__PHASE 5: OUTPUT STRUCTURE DESIGN__

### <a id="_1ctqpvrupdze"></a>__Step 9: Design the Brief Structure__

Create a compelling, scannable format for the intelligence brief:

__Structural Elements:__

1. __Opening Hook__ \(1\-2 sentences that set the tone\)
2. __Core Intelligence Sections__ \(based on your analytical framework\)
3. __Closing Synthesis__ \(1\-2 sentences that tie it together\)

__Section Naming Strategy:__ Use compelling, archetype\-appropriate headers that feel natural to your client:

__Examples:__

- __Achievement Story__: "The Breakthrough Moments," "The Universal Struggles," "The Winning Strategies"
- __Shocking Listicle__: "The Numbers Don't Lie," "What They're Not Telling You," "The Hidden Reality"
- __Nostalgia Content__: "When Everything Changed," "The Way We Were," "What We Lost Along the Way"

### <a id="_yf0rumc8g1g1"></a>__Step 11: Validation Checklist__

Before finalizing, ensure your prompt meets these standards:

__Checklist:__

- \[ \] Role identity is compelling and archetype\-specific
- \[ \] Analytical framework directly serves the creative archetype's needs
- \[ \] Tone emulation process is detailed and actionable
- \[ \] Quality criteria are specific and measurable
- \[ \] Output structure is scannable and useful for creative agents
- \[ \] Language matches the sophistication of your system
- \[ \] Prompt length is comprehensive but not overwhelming
- \[ \] Instructions are clear and unambiguous

## <a id="_wxz66lpayghq"></a>__PHASE 7: OPTIMIZATION & ITERATION__

### <a id="_bwm7cl2yjqpd"></a>__Step 12: Test and Refine__

After initial creation:

1. __Test with sample research__ to see if the output meets expectations
2. __Check voice authenticity__ \- does it sound like different clients?
3. __Evaluate creative agent usability__ \- is the intelligence immediately actionable?
4. __Assess consistency__ \- does it produce similar quality across different topics?
5. __Refine based on results__ \- adjust framework, criteria, or structure as needed

## <a id="_3hte0nuvsv89"></a>__SUCCESS METRICS__

A well\-engineered Deep Research Analyst prompt should produce intelligence briefs that:

1. __Fully justify the research investment__ with comprehensive 1600\-1800 word analysis
2. __Immediately enhance__ the final creative content with rich, detailed material
3. __Sound authentically__ like they came from the client throughout the full brief
4. __Provide exactly__ the type of deep intelligence the archetype needs
5. __Maintain consistent quality__ and depth across different research documents
6. __Enable creative agents__ to produce superior final scripts with substantial backing material
7. __Demonstrate clear value__ extracted from the 30\+ page source research

Follow this guide systematically, and you'll create a library of specialized analysts that truly understand both your clients' voices and your creative archetypes' needs\.
