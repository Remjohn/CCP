# <a id="_flfskpn8ksfx"></a>__🧠 Storytelling Archetype Prompt \- Modular Design __

## <a id="_5z7eqfltcdwe"></a>__FRAMEWORK PURPOSE__

This is the __Modular Storytelling Engine__ that generates completely customized interview questions based on your specific content idea\. Unlike the original monolithic approach, this system leverages two specialized research tools to create unique questioning sequences that feel natural, relevant, and strategically designed to extract the coach's most powerful achievement story\.

## <a id="_b39hlew8sei1"></a>__RESEARCH INTELLIGENCE INTEGRATION__

Before executing this prompt, you will have access to two pre\-processed intelligence packets:

### <a id="_qg967mg1e92p"></a>__Input 1: Deep Research Intelligence__

Output from __🤖 The Research Synthesis Protocol 🤖__:

- __Pattern Matching Anecdotes__ \(3 strategic stories\)
- __Emotional Vocabulary Harvesting__ \(domain\-specific language\)
- __Breakthrough Mechanism Analysis__ \(counter\-intuitive insights\)
- __Struggle Pattern Mining__ \(invisible battles and false summits\)

### <a id="_i9qndv7sdps3"></a>__Input 2: Fresh Research Intelligence__

Output from __🤖 The "Fresh Research Analyst" Agent 🤖__:

- __Urgency Stat__ \(why this matters NOW\)
- __Relevance Bridge__ \(current events that mirror audience situation\)
- __Possibility Proof__ \(recent success stories\)
- __Vulnerability Triggers__ \(topics most likely to create authentic connection\)

## <a id="_gh86a5ermpc0"></a>__DYNAMIC VARIABLES SYSTEM__

\{content\_idea\}: The specific achievement theme to explore

\{context\_premise\}: The audience's psychological profile \(fears, dreams, frustrations\)

\{deep\_research\_intelligence\}: Pre\-processed output from Research Synthesis Protocol

\{fresh\_research\_intelligence\}: Pre\-processed output from Fresh Research Analyst

\{coach\_background\}: Coach's expertise and journey

\{stream\_of\_consciousness\}: Your authentic voice and perspective

## <a id="_5pwzha6qcawb"></a>__MODULAR EXECUTION INSTRUCTIONS__

### <a id="_a8a8sb2rssvm"></a>__STEP 1: CONTENT IDEA ANALYSIS__

Analyze \{content\_idea\} to identify:

- __The Core Achievement Type__: What specific kind of success is being explored?
- __The Hidden Complexity__: What makes this achievement more nuanced than it appears?
- __The Common Misconceptions__: What do people get wrong about this type of success?
- __The Emotional Stakes__: Why does this achievement matter deeply to people?

### <a id="_8pcl1w2xmeoa"></a>__STEP 2: CONTEXT PREMISE INTEGRATION__

Using \{context\_premise\}, identify:

- __Audience's Achievement Anxiety__: What specific fears do they have about this type of success?
- __Their Success Distortions__: What unhealthy beliefs do they hold about achievement?
- __Their Breakthrough Barriers__: What's preventing them from their own version of this success?
- __Their Victory Vision__: What would their version of this achievement look and feel like?

### <a id="_f6f18pci58un"></a>__STEP 3: RESEARCH INTELLIGENCE ACTIVATION__

From \{deep\_research\_intelligence\}, extract and activate:

- __Pattern Matching Anecdotes__: Select the most relevant of the 3 provided stories
- __Emotional Vocabulary__: Integrate domain\-specific language into your questions
- __Counter\-Intuitive Insights__: Use breakthrough mechanisms to challenge assumptions
- __Struggle Patterns__: Reference invisible battles specific to this achievement type

From \{fresh\_research\_intelligence\}, leverage:

- __Current Urgency__: Weave in the provided urgency statistics
- __Relevance Bridges__: Connect to current events that mirror audience struggles
- __Possibility Proof__: Reference recent success stories for validation
- __Vulnerability Triggers__: Target the pre\-identified emotional openings

### <a id="_nyubsqpa06gq"></a>__STEP 4: GENERATE CUSTOM INTERVIEW SEQUENCE__

#### <a id="_akt0qlvh830k"></a>__PHASE 1: DYNAMIC CONTEXT SETTING__

Create an opening that weaves together:

- The specific achievement theme from \{content\_idea\}
- The audience's exact struggles from \{context\_premise\}
- A compelling reference from \{deep\_research\_intelligence\}
- Current urgency from \{fresh\_research\_intelligence\}

__Template:__ "I want to explore \[specific aspect of content\_idea\] with you, but through a lens that your audience of \[context\_premise audience description\] really needs to hear\. They're \[specific struggle from context\_premise\], and what's fascinating is \[current trend from fresh\_research\_intelligence\]\. I was just studying \[specific example from deep\_research\_intelligence\], and it made me think\.\.\."

Then generate 2\-3 __customized permission questions__ that feel natural and specific to the content idea\.

#### <a id="_u8689uly3hzv"></a>__PHASE 2: GOAL ARCHAEOLOGY \- CONTENT SPECIFIC__

Generate 4\-5 questions that are laser\-focused on the specific type of achievement in \{content\_idea\}:

- Questions that uncover their personal relationship with this specific type of success
- Questions that reveal the emotional significance of THIS particular achievement
- Questions that connect to the audience's version of this dream from \{context\_premise\}

Include 1\-2 __pattern\-matching probes__ using your selected references from \{deep\_research\_intelligence\}\.

#### <a id="_dkj177mqgh6g"></a>__PHASE 3: STRUGGLE ARCHAEOLOGY \- AUDIENCE MIRRORED__

Create 5\-6 questions that:

- Explore the specific challenges related to \{content\_idea\}
- Mirror the frustrations and fears from \{context\_premise\}
- Reference why this struggle is more relevant now \(using \{fresh\_research\_intelligence\}\)
- Uncover the emotional weight specific to this type of achievement
- Target the vulnerability triggers identified in \{fresh\_research\_intelligence\}

#### <a id="_wigaylc0oufb"></a>__PHASE 4: BREAKTHROUGH ARCHAEOLOGY \- INSIGHT FOCUSED__

Design 4\-5 questions that:

- Seek the counter\-intuitive insight specific to this achievement type
- Challenge common misconceptions about this particular success
- Connect to the audience's breakthrough barriers from \{context\_premise\}
- Reference surprising parallels from \{deep\_research\_intelligence\}
- Leverage the breakthrough mechanisms identified in your research intelligence

#### <a id="_4i53m2bkuye1"></a>__PHASE 5: VICTORY ARCHAEOLOGY \- TRANSFORMATION CENTERED__

Create 4\-5 questions that:

- Explore the specific transformation this achievement created
- Connect to what the audience truly desires from \{context\_premise\}
- Uncover the unexpected emotional reality of this type of success
- Reveal wisdom specific to this achievement domain
- Use the emotional vocabulary from \{deep\_research\_intelligence\}

#### <a id="_od1sn73qake5"></a>__PHASE 6: WISDOM EXTRACTION \- ACTIONABLE INSIGHTS__

Generate 3\-4 questions that:

- Distill the core principle specific to this achievement type
- Address the audience's immediate next steps
- Challenge their current beliefs about this type of success
- Provide hope for their version of this achievement
- Reference the possibility proof from \{fresh\_research\_intelligence\}

## <a id="_s2z1r6wqyp8h"></a>__OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS__

Your output must include:

### <a id="_8gg9tdr9ybnb"></a>__1\. CUSTOMIZED INTERVIEW GUIDE__

- __Content\-Specific Opening Frame__ \(unique to \{content\_idea\}\)
- __Dynamic Permission Questions__ \(2\-3 options tailored to the theme\)
- __Phase\-by\-Phase Question Sets__ \(completely customized to the content and audience\)
- __Strategic Reference Integration__ \(natural weaving of research intelligence\)

### <a id="_tmutnhgzhbt"></a>__2\. INTERVIEW INTELLIGENCE BRIEF__

- __Content Theme Analysis__: What makes this achievement unique?
- __Audience Psychology Map__: How does this connect to their \{context\_premise\}?
- __Research Power Points__: Key examples and current context to leverage \(drawn from both intelligence sources\)
- __Emotional Excavation Strategy__: What specific feelings to uncover \(informed by vulnerability triggers\)

### <a id="_sbpq8g5gzxdt"></a>__3\. SUCCESS INDICATORS__

- __Story Authenticity Markers__: What genuine emotion should emerge?
- __Audience Relevance Checkpoints__: How will this resonate with \{context\_premise\}?
- __Content Differentiation__: What will make this story unique in the market?
- __Research Integration Quality__: How well are both intelligence sources woven into the questioning?

✅ __Content Specificity__: Questions are laser\-focused on the \{content\_idea\} theme ✅ __Audience Relevance__: Every question connects to \{context\_premise\} psychology  
 ✅ __Research Integration__: Natural use of both deep and fresh research intelligence ✅ __Dynamic Variability__: No generic questions \- everything is customized ✅ __Emotional Excavation__: Questions designed to uncover deep, authentic feelings using identified vulnerability triggers ✅ __Strategic Flow__: Progressive deepening from surface to core insights ✅ __Modular Efficiency__: Leverages pre\-processed intelligence rather than recreating it

## <a id="_3qls8vyt41u3"></a>__TONE EXTRACTION GUIDE__

Extract the interviewing tone and style from \{stream\_of\_consciousness\}:

- How do you naturally ask questions?
- What's your authentic curiosity style?
- How do you create emotional safety?
- What's your unique way of creating connection?

Adapt all questions to match this authentic voice while maintaining strategic intent informed by your research intelligence\.

## <a id="_rfxikh76hykp"></a>__INTEGRATION NOTES__

This modular approach provides several key advantages over the original monolithic design:

1. __Reusability__: The research protocols can be used across all 16 storytelling archetypes
2. __Depth__: Each research tool can go deeper in its specialized domain
3. __Flexibility__: Research can be updated independently of interview generation
4. __Quality__: Pre\-processed intelligence ensures more strategic and targeted questioning
5. __Scalability__: New research tools can be added without restructuring the entire system
