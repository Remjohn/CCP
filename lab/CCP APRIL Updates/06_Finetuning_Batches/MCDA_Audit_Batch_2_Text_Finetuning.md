## 1. High Relevance (Integration Candidates - CCP / CCV / Sovereign Harness)

### 1. Fine-Grained Activation Steering Steering Less, Achieving More
* **MCDA Score:** 9.5/10
* **Key Concept:** AUSteer framework. Abandons coarse block-level steering in favor of Atomic Unit (AU) steering based on activation momentum.
* **CCP Alignment:** CRITICAL for CCV. By intervening at the atomic unit level rather than entire layers, we minimize the "brain damage" of blunt steering and preserve general capabilities, maximizing stylistic and creative control for script generation.
* **Action:** Integrate into the fundamental principles of the Eastern/CCV Activation Steering engine.

### 2. Guiding Giants Lightweight Controllers for Weighted Activation Steering in LLMs
* **MCDA Score:** 9.2/10
* **Key Concept:** Weighted Activation Steering (WAS). Uses a lightweight controller network to dynamically predict layer-specific weights and a global scalar for intervention at inference time.
* **CCP Alignment:** Highly synergistic with the OODA loop and agentic orchestration. Instead of static steering vectors, the Sovereign Harness can dynamically weight the intervention based on the prompt's context, maintaining high intelligence for benign reasoning while enforcing strong constraints when needed.
* **Action:** Adopt the lightweight controller concept for dynamic steering interventions.

### 3. HYPERSteer
* **MCDA Score:** 9.4/10
* **Key Concept:** Hypernetworks that generate steering vectors end-to-end conditionally based on natural language steering prompts and base LM activations.
* **CCP Alignment:** Phenomenal relevance for scaling CCV. Since CCV requires a combinatorial matrix of psychological constraints, generating steering vectors dynamically via hypernetworks allows infinite scalability without manually deriving individual vectors via unsupervised dictionary learning or extensive supervised tuning.
* **Action:** Adopt for the automated generation of psychological/stylistic steering variants.

### 4. Fine-Tuning with Divergent Chains of Thought Boosts Reasoning...
* **MCDA Score:** 8.8/10
* **Key Concept:** Diverse Chains of Thought (DCoT) within a *single* inference step to refine and self-correct reasoning intrinsically.
* **CCP Alignment:** Enhances the Agentic Harness. It fits perfectly into the deterministic Trigger-First OS by allowing the reasoning agent to generate multiple divergent chains and self-correct internally, increasing the precision of output evaluation.

### 5. Embedding Self-Correction as an Inherent Ability in Large Language Models...
* **MCDA Score:** 8.5/10
* **Key Concept:** Chain of Self-Correction (CoSC) via a two-phase fine-tuning approach (foundational + self-enhancement) for zero-shot self-correction.
* **CCP Alignment:** Directly applicable to engineering the Qwen 3.5 and Gemma 4 logic/reasoning agents within the sovereign stack.

### 6. Endogenous Resistance to Activation Steering in Language Models
* **MCDA Score:** 8.8/10
* **Key Concept:** Endogenous Steering Resistance (ESR). Models actively resist misaligned continuous steering.
* **CCP Alignment:** An essential safety/architecture consideration. When implementing CCV and AUSteer, we must design the steering vectors to be semantically congruent with the internal representation to avoid triggering ESR, ensuring smooth stylistic adaptation in video text.

### 7. Fine-Tuning Large Vision-Language Models as Decision-Making Agents...
* **MCDA Score:** 8.3/10
* **Key Concept:** RL fine-tuning of VLMs utilizing CoT reasoning to navigate multi-step environments.
* **CCP Alignment:** Relevant for CMF multimodal deconstruction. Moving beyond static vision models to RL-tuned, multi-step decision-making VLMs is key for hours-long video parsing and beat-accurate alignment.

### 8. Fine-Tuning Small Language Models for Domain-Specific AI
* **MCDA Score:** 8.0/10
* **Key Concept:** Shakti SLMs and quantization techniques (int4/int5) targeted at local, resource-constrained Edge AI deploy.
* **CCP Alignment:** Reaffirms the strategy of utilizing quantized SLMs (like Qwen3-4B) for high-efficiency logic tasks (batch scripts, rule checking) within the isolated sovereign architecture.
