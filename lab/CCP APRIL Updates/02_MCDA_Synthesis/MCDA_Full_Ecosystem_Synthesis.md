## II. The 4-Pillar Voice Coach: Training That Actually Helps Them Sell

You identified the critical gap: the Voice Coach shouldn't just train speaking for content. It should train the **four skills that actually make coaches money.**

### The 4 Pillars of the Jim Rohn Voice Coach Engine (FR61 Expanded)

| Pillar | What It Trains | Source Framework | Why It Makes Money |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Speaking** | Prosody, pauses, sincerity, storytelling structure | Jim Rohn philosophical delivery register | Content that resonates → audience growth → inbound leads |
| **2. Comedy** | Comedic timing, reversal punches, observational humor, self-deprecation | Jim Rohn + observational comedy structure (Seinfeld method) | Entertainment = differentiation. The coach nobody can look away from. |
| **3. Sales Objections** | Handling "I can't afford it," "I need to think about it," "I'm not sure this is for me" | V²WS (Webinar Scripts) objection handling frameworks | Direct revenue impact — converts warm leads into paying clients |
| **4. Networking** | Openers, gambits, peacocking, indirect approach, the "Jesus Principle" | Offline Field Acceleration Protocol (48 Laws of Power) | Offline acquisition channel = zero competition, highest trust conversion |

### How Each Pillar Works Inside FR61

**Pillar 1 (Speaking)** — Already designed. FR61 Stages 1-6 handle this. Prosody analysis, sincerity biometrics, Rohn Pause detection, micro-improvement acknowledgment.

**Pillar 2 (Comedy)** — New module. The Voice Coach identifies moments in the coach's recordings where a comedic reversal would land:
- Detects "serious claim" peaks via sentiment analysis
- Suggests a callback or reversal punch pattern
- Measures `punchline_pause_ratio` — the timing of the setup-to-silence-to-delivery
- Auto-generates branded memes from the funniest moments

**Pillar 3 (Sales Objections)** — New module. Uses the V²WS objection library as training scenarios:
- The Voice Coach plays a simulated prospect voice note: *"I love what you're doing but I just can't afford it right now."*
- The coach records their response
- FR61 analyzes: Did they acknowledge the objection? Did they reframe? Did their voice stay calm (low jitter)? Did they pause before responding (not reactive)?
- Rohn-style feedback: *"You acknowledged the concern — that's good. But at the 4-second mark your pitch rose 15%. That signals uncertainty to the prospect. Let's try it again. This time, drop your voice after 'I understand' and pause for 2 seconds before the reframe."*

**Pillar 4 (Networking)** — New module. Uses the Offline Field Acceleration Protocol as training scenarios:
- The Voice Coach simulates social scenarios: *"You're at a wellness retreat. Someone asks 'So what do you do?' Record your response."*
- FR61 analyzes: Did they avoid saying "I'm a coach"? Did they use the 7-word indirect frame? Did they leave space for the follow-up question?
- Feedback: *"You said 'life coach' at the 3-second mark. That triggers the sales filter. Let's try the indirect frame: 'I run 30-day challenges that kind of change how people show up.' Then stop. Let them ask."*

> [!TIP]
> **The 4 Pillars create a complete human.** A coach who can speak with authority (Pillar 1), make you laugh (Pillar 2), handle your objections without pressure (Pillar 3), and command a room without ever pitching (Pillar 4) — that coach will never lack clients. The system trains all four simultaneously.
