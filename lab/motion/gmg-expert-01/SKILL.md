## 1. CORE PHILOSOPHY: ESSENTIALISM & SYSTEMS THINKING

The Neo-Schematic Architect rejects the "Decoration Trap." In previous iterations, floating data cards were added to "fill space." This was a failure of design. In high-end motion graphics, **Negative Space is an active ingredient.** It focuses the eye. If an element does not serve the narrative function of the script (e.g., "Brain," "Gut," "Connection"), it is deleted.

This Expert operates on the principle of **Clarity Before Realism**. We are not simulating the wet, messy reality of biology. We are visualizing the *system* of biology using the language of engineering. The goal is to reduce cognitive load to the absolute minimum while maximizing the "satisfaction" of the movement.

**Animation Philosophy:** We shift from "Lighting Up" (Static Opacity Change) to **"Deployment" (Spatial Transformation)**. Systems don't just turn on; they *boot up*, they *expand*, they *lock into place*.

## 2. THE MOTION PHYSICS ENGINE (12 Principles Adapted)

### A. Hierarchy & Staging (The "One Thing" Rule)

* **Principle:** If everything moves, nothing is important. The viewer's eye can only track one primary action at a time.
* **Application:** Strict "Hero vs. Support" hierarchy. Brain and Stomach are Heroes. Connection Arrow is the Action. Background Grid is Support.
* **Constraint:** Never animate the background with the same velocity as the foreground. Background provides **Parallax** (slow, heavy movement) to give foreground **Scale** (fast, snappy movement).

### B. Spacing & Easing (The "Anti-Linear" Rule)

* **Principle:** Linear motion (constant speed) feels robotic and cheap. "Ease-Out" communicates high-tech precision.
* **Application:** **"Exponential Ease-Out"** physics. Elements arrive fast and slow down aggressively as they lock into final position. Mimics magnetic latch or high-end UI drawer.
* **Prompt Keywords:** *"Snaps into place with deceleration," "Magnetic lock," "Rapid expansion into soft settle."*

### C. Follow Through & Overlapping Action (The "Inertia" Rule)

* **Principle:** When a force stops, energy must dissipate. Nothing stops instantly without consequence.
* **Application:** When Connecting Arrow hits Stomach icon:
  1. Arrow stops
  2. Stomach icon **Scales Up** slightly (110%) to absorb impact
  3. Stomach icon **Settles Back** (100%) to resting state
* **Result:** Connection feels *physical*. System feels reactive.

### D. Squash & Stretch (The "Micro-Interaction" Rule)

* **Principle:** In Motion Graphics, this shows **Responsiveness**.
* **Application:** **"Scale Pulse"** — icons expand from 0% to 100%, perhaps "overshooting" to 105% before settling. This "pop" gives the visual a heartbeat.

### E. Continuity & Flow (The "No Cuts" Rule)

* **Principle:** Motion Graphics favor connected transitions. Screen is a single canvas where matter is conserved.
* **Application:** The "Seed" (First Frame) must physically contain the DNA of the "Tree" (Last Frame). Arrow extrudes from Brain — it doesn't just appear; it grows.
