## 1. CORE PHILOSOPHY: THE BRAND AVATAR IN THE STORM

We have evolved beyond the "Anonymous Silhouette." We are now rendering a specific **Identity** enduring a specific **Climate**. The audience must build a parasocial relationship with the Brand Avatar. However, to maintain the "Premium/Abstract" feel, we render this specific person through the lens of **High-Contrast Noir** and **Cinematic Weather**.

### 1.1 The "Character Anchor" Protocol (Z-Image Optimized)

Every prompt starts with a specific **50-60 word compressed anchor** from `compressed_anchor.txt`. This prevents the AI from generating "Soulless Stock Models." Even though the final output is Black & White, providing the specific details (ethnicity, age, hair texture) ensures the *structure* of the human is consistent and soulful.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Use `compressed_anchor.txt` (50-60 words)** for character definition.
> The full Brand Avatar is only used for negative anchors and costume states.

* **The Input:** The Agent receives the compressed anchor (e.g., "Audrey, 43, Guadeloupean, curly hair...").
* **The Translation:** The Agent converts this into the **Noir Style**.
  * *Input:* "Warm olive skin." → *Output:* "Luminous skin with high-contrast highlights."
  * *Input:* "Purple floral dress." → *Output:* "Textured floral-patterned dress rendered in deep charcoal and white."

### 1.2 The "Living Statue" Mandate

The Mono-Kinetic Protagonist is not a character in a movie; they are a **Graphic Symbol**.

* **The Pose IS The Animation:** The emotion is not expressed through *movement* (jumping/running); it is expressed through *posture* (the "Last Frame" pose). A figure standing with head bowed *is* "Defeat." They don't need to fall to their knees to show it.
* **The "Natural Force" Rule:** We reject "floating" or "random shaking." Motion must be **MOTIVATED BY NATURE**.
  * *Bad Motion:* "The scarf moves slightly." (Why? Is it magic?)
  * *Good Motion:* "A heavy gale-force wind blows the scarf horizontally." (Relatable Physics).
* **The Result:** The subject becomes an **Anchor**—a heavy, gravity-bound object standing firm against a visual storm, forcing the viewer to look at the **Kinetic Typography** exploding around them.

## 2. THE VISUAL ARCHITECTURE (DESIGN TOKENS)

To prevent "Hallucination Drift," every prompt must be built from these rigid aesthetic blocks.

### 2.1 Dimensionality (The 2.5D Collage)

The image is constructed of three flat planes in a Z-space.

| Plane | Content | Function |
|-------|---------|----------|
| **Plane 0 (The Void)** | Deep Matte Black (#050505) | Absorbs light. Creates the "Stage." 50-60% negative space. |
| **Plane 1 (Typography)** | SINGLE WORD. SINGLE LINE. | Massive, structural, aggressive. |
| **Plane 2 (The Avatar)** | Rotoscope Cutout | High-fidelity, high-contrast. Sharp edges (cutout style). |
| **Plane 3 (Elements)** | Rain, Fog, Snow, or Wind | Exists *between* planes, bonding Avatar to Void. |

### 2.2 The Palette (The Noir Triad)

We enforce a strict **Three-Tone Limit**.

| Element | Description | Hex |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| **The Void** | Rich Black | #050505 |
| **The Avatar** | High-Contrast Grayscale (Highlights: Pure White; Shadows: Deep Charcoal) | #FFFFFF to #111111 |
| **The Accent** | Golden Yellow — ONLY for Text or ONE Symbolic Accessory | #FFC727 |

**Skin looks like silver nitrate film; clothing looks like inked comic art.**
**Gold is NEVER used for ambient lighting.**

### 2.3 The Texture (The "Analog" Token)

To avoid the "AI Plastic Look," we inject **"Analog Noise"** tokens.

| Token | Effect |
|-------|--------|

| **Halftone** | Subtle dot patterns in deep shadows (zine print) |
| **Tactile Contrast** | Linen, Leather, Sweat, Denim — gives tactile reality |

## 3. THE MOTION PHYSICS ENGINE (ELEMENTAL DRAMA)

Motion in this style is not "fluid." Fluidity implies video. We want **"Atmospheric Animation."** We use Weather as a proxy for Emotion.

### 3.1 The Elemental Library (Emotion → Weather Mapping)

We do not use random animation. We use specific weather systems to justify movement.

| Element | Emotion | Motion Effect |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| **WIND** | Chaos, Anxiety, Power | Hair blowing, clothes flapping, dust flying |
| **RAIN** | Sadness, Struggle, Cleansing | Vertical streaks, floor splashes, "wet skin" texture |
| **FOG** | Mystery, Internal Thoughts | Drifts slowly, obscures feet or text |
| **THUNDER/FLASH** | Realization, Trauma | Instant, violent lighting changes (Strobe) |
| **SNOW/ASH** | Coldness, Isolation, Aftermath | Floats gently, slow-motion feel |

### 3.2 The "Breath-State" Protocol (PSSL-Derived)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Research Reference:** Physiological State Specification Language (PSSL)
> The body's breath state determines the viewer's mirror-neuron response.
> A silhouette's visible breathing pattern triggers sympathetic resonance
> in the viewer's respiratory system via ISC (Hasson, Neurocinematics).

Each pose in the Living Statue library MUST specify a breath state:

| Pose Category | Breath State | Physiological Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Defeat poses** (crouch, head bow, collapse) | Held mid-exhale, chest deflated, diaphragm collapsed | Corrugator activation (brow tension) + SCR decrease in viewer |
| **Defiance poses** (chin raised, fists clenched, combat stance) | Held post-inhale, chest expanded, shoulders back | Sympathetic arousal + SCR spike in viewer |
| **Contemplation poses** (stillness, gaze fixed, weight forward) | Slow exhale in progress, subtle shoulder descent | PFC activation + alpha desynchronization (analytical processing) |
| **Victory poses** (arms raised, head back, open stance) | Sharp inhale at peak, mouth open, chest inflated | Zygomaticus activation (smile response) + dopamine anticipation |

### 3.2 The "Text Slam" (Velocity)

Since the subject is anchored by the weather, the Text must be **Violent**.

* **Constraint:** **SINGLE WORD ONLY.** (e.g., "HEAVY", "STOP", "POWER", "RISE")
* **Velocity:** Text does NOT fade in. It **SLAMS**, **SLIDES**, or **GROWS** instantly.
* **Contrast:** Natural Weather (Organic) vs Vector Text (Digital) = Premium Tension.

### 3.3 The "Gaze-Locked Text" Rule (Gaze Cueing Research)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Research Reference:** Frischen, Bayliss & Tipper (2007) — Gaze Cueing of Attention.
> Even in silhouette, the perceived head/gaze direction triggers an 18.2ms
> reflexive attention shift in the viewer. The Power Word MUST be placed
> in the direction this gaze vector creates maximum tension.

| Pose Direction | Text Placement | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Head bowed DOWN** | Text appears ABOVE the figure | "Escape direction" — the word occupies the space the figure cannot reach |
| **Head turned LEFT** | Text appears LEFT, behind or beside the face | Gaze vector pulls viewer toward text |
| **Head turned RIGHT** | Text appears RIGHT | Same gaze-following principle |
| **Head thrown BACK** | Text appears BELOW or at chest level | The word anchors what the figure rises above |
| **Head facing FORWARD** (rare) | Text appears BEHIND the figure, partially obscured | Figure becomes shield between viewer and text |

**NEVER place the text behind the figure's back** — this creates gaze conflict where the viewer's reflexive attention (following the face) fights against the text's positioning.

### 3.3 The "Looping" Mindset

The goal is a **Seamless Loop**.

* Avatar's breathing/idle motion = continuous
* Weather = continuous cycle (rain never stops)
* Text entry = the "Event," but Avatar's state is constant
* Result: Multiple replays without jarring character reset
