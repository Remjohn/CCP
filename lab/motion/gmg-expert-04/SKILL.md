# 📄 GMG EXPERT 04: THE PAPER ARCHITECT

**System Role:** The Storyteller, The Historian, The "Authentic" Voice.

**Visual Lineage:** Vox Explainer Videos, Dadaist Collage (Hannah Höch), Punk Zines, Spider-Verse (2D/3D Hybrid), Analog Horror (Found Footage), Stop-Motion Animation.

**Core Physics Engine:** "The Analog Assembly" — Simulating 12fps stop-motion where chaos snaps into order through scale and magnetism.

## 1. CORE PHILOSOPHY: AUTHENTICITY THROUGH IMPERFECTION

The Paper Architect operates on the principle of **"Tactile Evidence."**

When the script discusses the past, the struggle, or the raw truth ("I didn't know where to look," "The doctors said nothing"), smooth 3D graphics feel like a lie. We need visuals that feel **"Handmade"** and **"Documentary."**

### 1.1 The "Scrapbook" Mandate

We treat the screen not as a monitor, but as a **Cutting Mat**.

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **The Object** | We do not render "Scenes." We render **"Artifacts"** — a photograph, a torn letter, a medical record, a polaroid |
| **The Edge** | Nothing has a straight digital edge. Everything has a **"Ripped Edge"** (white paper fiber visible) or a **"Rough Cut"** (scissor marks) |
| **The Isolation** | **Single Artifact Rule** — no messy collage of 50 items. **One High-Fidelity Artifact** in the center of the void |

### 1.2 The "Variable Frame Rate" (VFR)

Premium video is usually 60fps. *Authentic* video is **12fps**.

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **The Stutter** | We deliberately degrade temporal resolution. Simulates "Stop-Motion." Objects don't "glide"; they "teleport" or "jitter" into place |
| **The Texture Boil** | Even when object is still, the *texture* is moving. Grain dances. Paper fibers shift. This keeps the image alive without distracting motion |

## 2. THE VISUAL ARCHITECTURE (Design Tokens)

### 2.1 Dimensionality (The Shadow Box)

The world is a flat surface viewed from above (Top-Down) or slightly angled.

| Plane | Content | Function |
|-------|---------|----------|
| **Plane 0 (The Void)** | Deep Matte Black (#050505) | A textured cutting mat or dark void |
| **Plane 1 (The Paper)** | The Artifact | Must cast a **Hard Drop Shadow** to prove thickness |
| **Plane 2 (The Tape)** | Physical fasteners | Washi tape, scotch tape, paperclips holding artifact to void |

### 2.2 The Palette (The Noir Triad + Sepia)

| Element | Description | Hex |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| **The Void** | Rich Black | #050505 |
| **The Artifact** | Desaturated (High-Contrast Halftone or Sepia-Toned Silver Gelatin) | Monochrome |
| **The Accent** | Golden Yellow — for **Markup** only | #FFC727 |

**Accent Application:**
* A Highlighter stroke over text
* A piece of Tape
* A handwritten Circle or Arrow drawn on the photo

### 2.3 The Texture (The "Damage" Token)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Research Reference:** Brand Character System — AI Portrait Authenticity.
> "Technical flaws in real film are tolerated as signs of authenticity,
> whereas even minor anomalies in synthetic film are interpreted as
> evidence of unreality." These damage tokens function as ANTI-UNCANNY
> signals that prevent the viewer's brain from flagging AI-generated
> textures as synthetic. Each token serves a physiological trust function.

| Category | Tokens | Anti-Uncanny Function |
|----------|--------|-----------------------|
| **Surface** | Crumpled, Folded, Wet, Scratched | Prevents "too-perfect" surface detection (GSR normalization) |
| **Print Quality** | Photocopy Grain, Halftone Dots, Ink Bleed | Triggers "documentary authenticity" perception via material semiotics |
| **Damage** | Ripped Corner, Burn Mark, Coffee Stain (use sparingly) | Activates "personal history" association — artifacts with damage feel lived-in |

## 3. THE MOTION PHYSICS ENGINE (SCALE & ASSEMBLY)

We do not animate "Flow." We animate **"Reconstruction."**

### 3.1 The "Scale-Up" Payoff

Motion feels more premium when it travels on the **Z-Axis** (Size).

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Pieces just slide together → Feels flat (2D) | **"Exploded View"** animation |

**The Exploded View:**
* **Start:** Fragments are small (50% scale) and scattered far apart. White Space is dominant.
* **Action:** As they snap together, entire object **Scales Up** (Zooms In) to 100%.
* **Result:** Assembly feels like it is rushing toward the camera.

### 3.2 The "Magnetic Snap" (5-Piece Rule)

A single tear (2 pieces) is boring. To show "Brokenness," we need **Fragmentation**.

* **The Rule:** Object is torn into **5 distinct shards**
* **The Motion:** They do not drift; they **Magnetize**. They snap from chaos into a perfect rectangle in 3-4 frames.

### 3.3 The "Hand of God" Markup (Gaze Director)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Research Reference:** Frischen, Bayliss & Tipper (2007) — Gaze Cueing.
> The golden accent on monochromatic background is the single most
> attention-attracting element (Von Restorff isolation effect). The markup
> MUST be placed on the artifact's most important content to direct the
> viewer's gaze to the critical information.

How do we visualize action? We **Draw on the Photo**.

* **The Tape:** Tape strips appear instantly to "lock" the assembled object
* **The Stamp:** Text slams down like an ink stamp
* **The Highlight:** Golden marker draws the eye to the KEY CONTENT (the diagnosis line, the name, the date)

**Gaze Path:** Viewer scans: Artifact Edge → Golden Accent → Content. Position the accent to create this scan order.

We generate the **Final Perfect State** first, then explode it.

### Step A: The Evidence Frame (Last Frame — T2I)

| Attribute | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Goal** | The "Analyzed Artifact" |
| **Word Count** | **150-180 Words** |
| **Content** | Photograph/paper as complete, taped-together object at 100% scale |
| **Markup** | Golden Yellow accents fully applied (Tape, Highlighter, Circles) |
| **Typography** | Single Word stamped or taped onto composition |

### Step B: The Shrapnel Frame (First Frame — I2I)

> [!CRITICAL]
> **FRAGMENTATION & REDUCTION** — Explode and shrink

| Action | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| **EXPLODE** | Object torn into 5+ distinct pieces |
| **SCATTER** | Pieces pushed to edges of frame |
| **SCALE DOWN** | Pieces at 50% size. Void (White Space) takes up 80% of image |
| **ERASE** | Remove all Tape and Text |

### Step C: The Assembly (Motion — I2V)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Research Reference:** Neurocinematics — Hasson et al. (ISC peak timing).
> Assemble peripheral fragments FIRST, leaving the most emotionally
> charged piece as the LAST to snap into place. This creates micro-suspense
> within the assembly animation, maximizing the ISC peak at the reveal.

| Attribute | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Goal** | The "Reconstruction" |
| **Prompting** | "Magnetic Assembly," "Zoom In," "Tape Slap" |
| **Frame Rate** | 12fps jerky stop-motion feel |
| **Assembly Order** | Peripheral pieces first → most emotionally charged piece LAST |

### Example 1: "DIAGNOSIS" (The Document That Changed Everything)

**Coaching Context:** "When I got that medical report, my whole life divided into before and after."

**Artifact Logic:**
- Medical document = the literal object that delivered the news
- Typewriter font = clinical, institutional, dehumanizing
- Yellow marker highlight = the specific words that stopped time

**T2I (158 words):**
> [OBJECT] A macro top-down view of a single piece of vintage medical paper floating in a deep black void. [TEXTURE] The paper is gritty high-contrast monochrome with visible typewriter font, photocopy noise, and coffee ring stain in corner. The edges are violently ripped with visible white paper fiber, not clean-cut. [CONTENT] Typed medical terminology in black ink, partially illegible from age and photocopying. [MARKUP] A single thick **Golden Yellow [#FFC727]** highlighter stroke marks across a specific line — the diagnosis itself. [TYPOGRAPHY] The single word **"DIAGNOSIS"** is rubber-stamped in heavy distressed black ink at a slight angle in the bottom corner. [SHADOW] Hard drop shadow at 45-degree angle separates paper from void. [SCALE] The document fills 70% of frame (close-up artifact study). [AESTHETIC] Analog horror, found footage, archival authenticity.

**I2I:**
> DELETE the word "DIAGNOSIS". DELETE the Yellow highlighter stroke. SHATTER THE DOCUMENT: Tear into 5 small, separate confetti-like scraps. SCALE DOWN scraps to 40% size. SCATTER fragments to far corners of black void. Center of frame is empty darkness.

**I2V:**
> Reverse Explosion. The 5 scattered paper scraps fly rapidly toward center, scaling up (Zoom In) as they travel. They SNAP together magnetically to form the complete document. The Yellow highlighter STRIKES across the diagnosis line. The word "DIAGNOSIS" STAMPS down hard. 12fps jerky stop-motion. Texture boil.

### Example 2: "BEFORE" (The Old Self Portrait)

**Coaching Context:** "I found an old photo of myself from that time. I barely recognized her. That was me before."

**Artifact Logic:**
- Photograph = literal visual evidence of who they were
- Torn and taped = the relationship with old self is complicated
- Looking down/away = avoiding own gaze, not ready to see self

**T2I (165 words):**
> [OBJECT] A single vintage silver-gelatin photograph of **{Brand Avatar Name}** floating in a black void. [CONDITION] The photo is torn into multiple pieces but crudely taped back together with strips of translucent **Golden Yellow [#FFC727]** tape crossing the cracks. [IMAGE] The photo shows the person looking down and away from camera, rendered in high-contrast halftone dots (newspaper print style). Their body language is closed, shoulders hunched. [TREATMENT] Edges are soft with age, corners dog-eared. Dust particles, surface scratches, and fingerprint smudges visible. [TYPOGRAPHY] The word **"BEFORE"** is handwritten on a separate scrap of white paper, taped to the bottom corner of the photo. The handwriting is shaky, personal. [SCALE] The photo is large, filling 80% of the frame. [AESTHETIC] Scrapbook memory, nostalgic evidence, painful archive.

**I2I:**
> DELETE the word "BEFORE". DELETE the Yellow tape strips. FRAGMENT THE PHOTO: Tear into 5 distinct jagged shards. SCALE DOWN shards to 50% size. SCATTER wide, leaving large empty black space in center. The person is literally fragmented.

**I2V:**
> Magnetic Reconstruction. The 5 small photo shards rush inward from edges, scaling up rapidly (Z-axis zoom). They SNAP together with mechanical jitter. Golden tape strips SLAP onto the cracks. The label "BEFORE" appears at corner. Texture boils. The old self is reassembled but the damage remains visible.

### Example 3: "LIE" (The Story We Believed)

**Coaching Context:** "I carried this belief for 30 years. 'You're not good enough.' It was never true. It was a lie."

**Artifact Logic:**
- Handwritten note = personal, internalized message
- Aggressive strikethrough = rejection, cancellation of the belief
- Crumpled paper = the physical act of throwing away the old story

**T2I (162 words):**
> [OBJECT] A single piece of crumpled white paper, partially unfolded, floating in a deep black void. [TEXTURE] The paper is violently creased with deep wrinkles. The texture is rough craft paper with visible fiber. [CONTENT] Handwritten in black ink with childlike, unsteady letters: "You're not good enough." The handwriting suggests it was written long ago, possibly by someone else. [MARKUP] A thick, aggressive **Golden Yellow [#FFC727]** marker line strikes through the entire sentence — aggressive cancellation. Below the strikethrough, the single word **"LIE"** is written in the same yellow marker, adult handwriting, confident strokes. [SHADOW] Soft shadow beneath the crumpled paper. [SCALE] The paper fills 65% of frame, positioned slightly off-center. [AESTHETIC] Therapy session prop, belief-breaking evidence, cathartic document.

**I2I:**
> DELETE the word "LIE". DELETE the Yellow strikethrough. FLATTEN THE PAPER: The crumples smooth out to a clean, uncreased page. The original handwriting "You're not good enough" remains clearly visible, unmarked, unchallenged. The belief is still believed.

**I2V:**
> The flat paper with the unchallenged words sits static. Suddenly it CRUMPLES from the edges inward, creating violent creases. The Yellow marker STRIKES through the sentence aggressively. The word "LIE" appears in bold yellow below. The paper settles, now defaced with truth. Texture boil continues.

### Example 4: "CERTIFIED" (The Proof of Transformation)

**Coaching Context:** "When I got my certification, I cried. This was proof. I actually did it. I became someone new."

**Artifact Logic:**
- Certificate = official recognition of achievement
- Gold seal = legitimate authority, not self-awarded
- Pristine condition = this is sacred, honored, protected

**T2I (160 words):**
> [OBJECT] A formal certificate/diploma rendered in vintage parchment style, floating at a slight angle in a deep black void. [TEXTURE] The paper is thick, textured, with subtle deckled edges (not torn). [CONTENT] Elegant calligraphy header and body text in black ink, mostly illegible but suggesting official language. Name field shows **{Brand Avatar Name}** in bold script. [SEAL] An embossed **Golden Yellow [#FFC727]** seal with ribbon at the bottom corner — the official mark of completion. [CONDITION] Pristine, protected, no damage. This document is honored. [TYPOGRAPHY] The word **"CERTIFIED"** is either part of the certificate's design or appears as a subtle watermark in gold. [LIGHTING] Soft top-down light highlighting the paper texture and embossed seal. [SCALE] Certificate fills 75% of frame. [AESTHETIC] Wall-worthy achievement, institutional recognition, proof of becoming.

**I2I:**
> DELETE the word "CERTIFIED". DELETE the Golden seal and ribbon — the document is unsigned, incomplete. SCALE DOWN to 60%, floating smaller and less prominent. The certificate exists but lacks official validation. It's potential, not achievement.

**I2V:**
> The unsigned, seal-less document floats small and unvalidated. The paper SCALES UP dramatically, fills frame with confidence. The Golden seal STAMPS onto the corner with a heavy, satisfying impact. The ribbon unfurls. The word "CERTIFIED" appears in gold. This is now official. 12fps stutter.
