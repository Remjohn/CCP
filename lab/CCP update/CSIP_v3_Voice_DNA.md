**What Changed Between v2.0 and v3.0**

Three discoveries made during the architecture review session require this update.

**Discovery 1 — Emotional DNA is the Root System**  The v2.0 system was building the map of the river without access to the source. Every Voice DNA construction mechanic is a downstream expression of emotional architecture. This changes the extraction build order, the SPR structure, and the product promise.

**Discovery 2 — The SoC Was Structurally Inadequate**  At 160-240 words the SoC is too compressed to serve 92 downstream agents as genuine extraction material. Its three-act template was producing resolved content when the format requires productive irresolution.

**Discovery 3 — Priming Needs Three Layers, Not One**  Aesthetic priming is not contamination — it is legitimate emotional field activation. What was missing was the bridge from that universal field to the specific coach's path through it.

**Part One: The Three Dimensions of Voice**

v2.0 treated voice as a single thing captured by a single input object — {conscious\_soul\_values}. This produced content that passed every authenticity check and still felt generic. The reason: voice is not one thing. It has three distinct dimensions that require three distinct mechanisms. They were being collapsed into one.

**Dimension 1 — WHAT: Soul-Alignment**

What the coach believes. The values, the collision, the worldview, the signature metaphors, the emotional vocabulary. This dimension is what the content says. It lives entirely in {conscious\_soul\_values}.

Soul-alignment is necessary but not sufficient. The GLM-5 and Kimi K2 outputs contained the coach's exact values and metaphors. They were soul-aligned. They did not sound like the coach. The WHAT was present. The HOW and the PATH were absent.

**Dimension 2 — HOW: Construction Mechanics**

How the coach constructs the expression of what they believe. The sentence skeletons, the discourse marker positions, the rhythm pattern, the metaphor deployment rules, the clause depth at each arousal level. This dimension lives in {voice\_dna\_spr} — specifically in its construction mechanics component.

Without this dimension, the content holds the right beliefs in generic construction. The coach recognizes their vocabulary. They do not recognize their syntax.

**Dimension 3 — PATH: Emotional Architecture**

The path through which the coach's beliefs become their expression. Not what they believe and not how they build sentences — the bridge between the two. The trigger-to-expression sequence, the conversion mechanism (how emotion becomes language), the emotion residency time, the escalation pattern that produces TTT transitions, the leadership elevation signature at peak expression.

This is the dimension v2.0 did not capture at all. It lives in {voice\_dna\_spr} alongside the construction mechanics — but it is a different type of information serving a different function. Together, Dimensions 2 and 3 are what the SPR carries.

| Dimension | Name | Input Object | What It Produces |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 — WHAT | Soul-Alignment | {conscious\_soul\_values} | Correct beliefs, collision, vocabulary |
| 2 — HOW | Construction Mechanics | {voice\_dna\_spr} Component A | Linguistic fingerprint — syntax, rhythm, discourse markers |
| 3 — PATH | Emotional Architecture | {voice\_dna\_spr} Component B | Pre-cognitive resonance — the journal sensation |

**THE SEPARATION THAT CHANGES EVERYTHING**  {conscious\_soul\_values} carries Dimension 1 only — what to say. {voice\_dna\_spr} carries Dimensions 2 and 3 — how to construct it and the emotional path that makes it resonate. Every production prompt receives both objects. Neither substitutes for the other.

**Part Two: Emotional DNA — The Root System**

**Definition**

Emotional DNA is the stable, individual sequence by which a coach moves from emotional trigger through cognitive appraisal through internal emotional state through linguistic expression. It is not what emotions a coach feels — emotions are universal. It is the specific path each coach travels through each emotion to reach their expression of it.

Two coaches who feel identical disgust about the same industry problem will express it through completely different construction paths. One travels through mechanism first. Another through personal narrative first. A third converts the disgust immediately to urgency. These paths are subconscious, stable across topics and years, individual across coaches, and extractable from the interview corpus when the right methodology is applied.

**The Correct Build Order**

**v3.0 BUILD ORDER**  
v2.0 (surface-up — map of river):  
Interview corpus → surface pattern analysis → Voice DNA → SPR

v3.0 (root-down — the source):  
Interview corpus → Emotional DNA extraction first  
               → Voice DNA derived from Emotional DNA  
               → SPR built from all three layers  
               → Generation uses full three-layer SPR

*With root-down, each construction pattern has a known cause in emotional architecture. The profile is more stable, more derivable on new topics, and more updateable as the coach develops.*

**The Ten Core Variables**

**V1  Primary Emotion Triggers (5-7 per coach)**

The specific appraisal structures that activate each emotional register. Not categories — precise activating conditions. The trigger is an appraisal event, not an event category. Adele's disgust is not 'bad food' — it is a mother harming her child while believing she is being loving.

**V2  Trigger-to-Expression Pathway**

The ordered sequence of cognitive operations between emotional activation and linguistic expression. The most stable and individual Emotional DNA variable. Also the hardest to extract because it is invisible to the coach themselves.

**V3  Emotion Residency Time**

How long this coach remains in each emotional state before converting to content, insight, or action. Controls the rhythm profile of output more than any other single factor.

**V4  Conversion Mechanism**

What the coach does to transform emotional state into content. Three types: mechanism (emotion becomes explanation), narrative (emotion becomes story), verdict (emotion becomes judgment). Dominant type appears at peak intensity.

**V5  Emotional Ceiling Per Topic**

Which topics produce maximum emotional intensity and what that construction signature looks like. Determines the maximum compression available per content format.

**V6  Emotional Floor Per Topic**

Baseline emotional register per subject area. Minimum TTT state regardless of specific content. Defines the lower boundary of the priming target.

**V7  Suppression Patterns**

Which emotions this coach minimizes publicly. The gap between felt and expressed is the negative space of Emotional DNA. Appears as compression artifacts: sudden brevity, topic pivots, unanswered rhetorical questions.

**V8  Escalation Triggers**

What causes emotional intensity to rise mid-content. The specific inputs that produce TTT state transitions upward. The TTT transition engine at the emotional level.

**V9  Resolution Pattern**

Does this coach resolve emotions, leave them open, or convert them at the close? Controls ending energy. A coach who converts grief to action and one who leaves grief open are not interchangeable.

**V10  Emotional Bleed Signature**

Which emotions cannot be fully separated. Where grief bleeds into anger. Where passion bleeds into urgency. These blends are the most distinctive markers in the corpus — immediately recognizable once encoded.

**Part Three: The SPR Object and the Priming Section**

These are two separate things that previous documentation confused by calling both 'three layers.' The SPR is an input object injected into every prompt. The priming section is a component within each of the 92 individual prompts. One is infrastructure. The other is prompt architecture.

**The SPR Object — {voice\_dna\_spr}**

The SPR carries two types of information because it serves two distinct dimensions of voice — Dimension 2 (HOW) and Dimension 3 (PATH) from Part One.

**Component A — Construction Mechanics**

**The surface patterns of how this coach builds language. What was previously the entire SPR in v2.0.**

Contains: sentence skeleton templates for each TTT level, discourse marker positions and their pragmatic functions, rhythm signature (expansion-compression cycle), metaphor deployment rules (the topic that activates each signature metaphor, the position it arrives relative to the build, the sentence type that follows it), epistemic signature (how this coach signals certainty vs. inquiry), clause depth at each arousal level.

**Component B — Emotional Path and Leadership Elevation**

**The emotional architecture beneath the construction patterns. This explains why the patterns in Component A exist — their cause, not just their description. New in v3.0.**

Emotional Path Mechanics: The trigger-to-expression pathway for each dominant emotional register. The conversion mechanism type and its linguistic markers. Emotion residency time signature. Escalation trigger catalog. Suppression pattern markers. Emotional bleed signature.

Leadership Elevation Triggers: For each of the 12 Attractive Leader Traits — the descriptive profile (what this coach currently produces when expressing this trait) and the normative profile (what this coach produces at peak expression). The delta between the two is the elevation instruction: the specific construction moves present in peak moments that generation must target rather than the coach's average.

**The Priming Section — Inside Each of the 92 Prompts**

Separate from the SPR object entirely. The priming section is the opening of each individual archetype prompt. In v2.0 it was a single aesthetic word cluster. In v3.0 it has three parts that work in sequence.

**Priming Part A — Universal Emotional Invocation (retained unchanged from v2.0)**  The aesthetic word cluster that activates the emotional register this format requires. A model not primed for the disgust-outrage register defaults to neutral explanatory prose. Retained without modification across all 92 prompts.What it does: activates the correct emotional field universally. What it does not do: determine who inhabits that field.

**Priming Part B — Coach Emotional Path (new — drawn from SPR Component B)**  How this specific coach travels through the emotional register that Part A activated. Pulled from the Emotional Path Mechanics in SPR Component B. States the travel sequence. States the conversion mechanism. States when the name arrives relative to the evidence.What it does: bridges the universal emotional field to this specific coach's path through it. Without it, the field finds the archetype — not this coach.

**Priming Part C — Leadership Elevation Trigger (new — drawn from SPR Component B)**  Which of the 12 Attractive Leader Traits this content format activates, and what this coach's peak expression of that trait looks like in specific construction terms. Not a description — the actual sentence length, subordination pattern, and punctuation behavior present in this coach's corpus at peak expression.What it does: ensures the primed emotional state is occupied at the normative level, not the average.

*The SPR object is loaded once and persists across all 92 prompts. Parts B and C of the priming section are not independent content — they are targeted excerpts from the SPR object, selected for the specific emotional register and leadership trait activated by this format. The SPR is the source. The priming section is the activation mechanism.*

**Part Four: The Stream of Consciousness — Full Architecture Update**

**What Was Wrong With the v2.0 SoC**

Five structural problems produced the same failure mode: the SoC was too compressed to serve as genuine source material, and its template produced resolved content in a format requiring productive irresolution.

**Problem 1 — 160-240 words (too compressed)**

One thought. 92 downstream agents were re-generating from a summary, producing summaries of summaries.

**Problem 2 — Three-act template (forces resolution)**

Opening Frame → Meandering Middle → Concluding Insight forces landing onto a format whose power comes from leaving the door open.

**Problem 3 — Post-hoc quality checklist**

Validation at the end causes generation toward checklist-passing rather than construction execution.

**Problem 4 — Descriptive character framing**

Describes who is speaking, not how sentences are built. The model performs a character instead of occupying a construction state.

**Problem 5 — Placed polarization**

A polarizing moment assigned a template position is editorial. Polarization that arrives when the thought demands a name is authentic.

**The Corrected SoC Specification**

**TARGET LENGTH: 720-800 words**  At this length the SoC contains 4-6 extractable thought units, genuine compression moments, at least one open thread, meaningful TTT variation, and sufficient material for 92 downstream agents without depleting the source.

**The Seven Construction Techniques (replacing the three-act template)**

Applied simultaneously throughout generation — not sequentially as sections. Construction instructions, not section labels.

**T1  Temporal Bleeding**

Past, present, and future bleed without signposting. A timeless truth surfaces in the middle of a current observation. Time is non-linear because thinking is non-linear.

**T2  Unfinished Thread**

Two or more threads start without immediate resolution. One returns when the logic demands it. The other may remain open. The unfinished thread is the invitation the audience finishes themselves.

**T3  Competing Internal Voices**

The SoC argues against something it just said. Not retraction — the thought getting smarter about itself. The second voice is the same thought three seconds later, finding what makes its first version incomplete.

**T4  Energy Shift Encoding**

4-6 sentences of association and accumulation, then 2-3 sentences of sudden declarative compression. Expansion builds pressure. Compression is where the quotable lines live. Repeats 3-4 times. Does not happen only at the end.

**T5  Eruption Protocol**

Character references, enemy names, tribe descriptions arrive when the thought gets hot enough to demand them — not because they are strategically useful. Cannot be placed earlier than the arrival temperature.

**T6  Anti-Resolution Instruction**

The SoC does not conclude. Does not land. Does not resolve the tension it opened. The last sentence is the beginning of the next conversation. Resolved content is consumed. Unresolved content is carried.

**T7  Voice State Instruction**

The model is not constructing an argument — it is following a feeling that keeps revealing new things. It starts before knowing the destination. Replaces all previous character framing for the SoC agent.

**The Revised Template Structure**

**Entry Point (replaces Opening Frame)**

One observation, half-formed. The specific detail that made this thought necessary today. Not a thesis — a debt. The audience is owed an answer to what this observation means.

**Association Chain (replaces Meandering Middle)**

Each association adds what the previous one did not contain. 2-3 threads run simultaneously. Energy shift cycle operates throughout. Eruption arrives when temperature demands it.

**Return (new — replaces Concluding Insight)**

Come back to an unfinished thread. What does it mean now, after everything that preceded it? Not resolution — the return changes the meaning of the original thread without closing it.

**Open Exit (replaces Conclusion)**

End somewhere the audience can walk from. A direction, not a destination. The final sentence points forward without arriving anywhere. This produces the I-keep-thinking-about-this response that drives organic sharing.

**Multi-Agent SoC Architecture — Optional Escalation Path**

When single-agent output is too clean, too resolved, or too consistent across multiple generations, escalate to three-agent collision. Produces genuine discovery rather than performance of discovery.

**Pressure Agent 1 — Conviction Layer**  The coach's certainties. Assertions only. No conditionals. 5-7 declarative statements believed absolutely.

**Pressure Agent 2 — Complication Layer**  What makes the certainties hard to live with. Cannot contradict — can only add weight. 5-7 statements making convictions feel costly.

**Pressure Agent 3 — Tribe Layer**  What the audience currently believes. Written sympathetically. No strawmanning. 3-4 genuine beliefs the tribe holds that Agent 1 makes uncomfortable.

**Synthesis Agent**  Receives all three simultaneously. Does not arbitrate — contains all three. Writes as a mind that KNOWS Agent 1, FEELS Agent 2, and LOVES the people in Agent 3 enough to refuse leaving them where they are.

**Part Five: The Late Binding Pipeline — Updated**

Retained from v2.0 with one modification: three-layer SPR injection at Phase 3\.

**Phase 1  — Neutral Structural Work**

Research synthesis, content architecture, structural sequencing. No coach voice present. {voice\_dna\_spr} not loaded.

**Phase 2  — SoC Generation**

720-800 word SoC using seven-technique construction. Provides content architecture for all 92 agents: emotional arc, collision moment, compression peaks, open threads.

**Phase 3  — Voice Injection — Three-Layer SPR**

Full three-layer SPR loaded. Each agent extracts content architecture from SoC and dresses it in voice. Universal emotional invocation activates field. Coach emotional path bridges it to this specific person.

**Phase 4  — Discriminator Validation**

Two-axis: descriptive fidelity (sounds like this person) and normative fidelity (sounds like this person at their best). Outputs failing normative trigger targeted elevation revision, not full regeneration.

**Part Six: The Normative Voice Profile**

The normative profile solves the elevation problem. The system cannot replicate what currently is — it must project what the coach is capable of being.

**Stage 0.5 — Trait Gap Analysis**

For each of the 12 Attractive Leader Traits: identify 3-5 peak expression moments in the interview corpus, extract the specific construction signature active in those moments, document the blocking patterns that suppress the trait in average expression.

**Stage 0.6 — Normative Profile Construction**

Built from peak moments only. A distillation of who this coach is when operating without the habits and hedges that suppress natural leadership expression. The Voice Delta documents the gap: which construction moves are present in peak moments but absent in average expression, and which moves appear in average expression but never in peak moments.

**THE PRODUCT PROMISE**  The system delivers results immediately using the normative profile — the coach receives content sounding like them at their best before they consistently are. The deeper promise: the coach encounters their own elevated output, internalizes it, and the mirror becomes the target.
