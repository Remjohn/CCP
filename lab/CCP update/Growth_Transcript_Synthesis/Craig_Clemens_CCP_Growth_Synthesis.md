*Resolving contradictions without making compromises.*

- *CCP Synthesis (The "Synthesized Authority" feature):* We build a prompt architecture inside the CCP content generator that forces the coach to borrow authority. - *The Resolution (Principle 3 - Local Quality):* Change the structure so different parts serve different functions.
- *CCP Synthesis:* The Top-of-Funnel marketing handles the Dream. The Telegram DMs handle the Reality. ## 6. CBAR (Constraint-Based Adversarial Reasoning)

*Stress-testing the "7 Hijacks" against modern consumer skepticism.*

**The Adversarial Challenge:**

They *know* they are being marketed to. If a coach tries to execute a PT Barnum stunt or a Dr. Oz balloon-popping routine on TikTok, they will be instantly roasted in the comments as a "cringe scammer." 

**The Constraint:** 
You cannot use overt, cheesy 1990s direct-response tactics on Gen Z or Millennials. The 7 Hijacks are valid, but the *delivery mechanism* must be stealthy, ironic, or hyper-authentic.

**The Resolution (The Irony / ASMR Stealth Integration):**
Clemens gave us the exact solution in his transcript regarding ASMR. He took a video of marbles rolling down stairs and breaking glass (which people watch purely for the satisfying physics) and *hijacked* the end of the video with his product.
*   *CCP Execution:* We train our coaches to use the "Stealth Hijack." Do not make a video that looks like an ad. Make a video that looks like pure, satisfying entertainment. 
    1. A coach uploads a video of themselves doing something physically satisfying or difficult (e.g., perfectly parallel parking a vintage truck, making a perfect pour-over coffee in silence). 
    2. CCP processes this "ASMR" hook. 
    3. At second 15, the trap springs. The coach delivers the intellectual killshot. 
    We use the physics of the demonstration (the ASMR satisfaction) to freeze the viewer's limbic system, allowing the coach to bypass the logical "ad-blocker" area of the brain.
