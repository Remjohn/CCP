FAQ: MEME ORCHESTRATOR V3
Q: Why exactly 6 memes instead of 12 or more?
A: Strategic restraint. The README.md makes it clear:
"Memes inject personality and pattern interrupts without oversaturation. 6 is the sweet spot between impact and professionalism."
Too many memes compete with your 12 visual hooks (which tell the protagonist's transformation story). 6 memes provide strategic pattern interrupts without making the webinar feel like a TikTok compilation.
Q: What if a meme scores below 28/40 on the rubric?
A: Replace it using the backup theory. Each directive includes a "backup_theory" recommendation for this scenario. Example:
Original: Benign Violation meme for Hook (Slot 1)
Score: 24/40 (too polarizing for this tribe)
Replacement: Relief Theory meme (safer emotional connection)
New Score: 32/40 ✅
Q: Can I create custom meme formats instead of using templates?
A: Yes, but ensure:
1:1 ratio (1080x1080px) - Maintains V²WS canvas consistency
High contrast text - Readable on 5-inch phone screens
Tribal relevance - Inside jokes/references tribe recognizes
Conversion alignment - Serves Jason's emotional arc
Custom memes often score higher on Tribal Resonance but require more time to create. Use templates for speed, custom for maximum impact.
Q: What if my tribe doesn't respond to humor at all?
A: Rare, but possible for highly formal audiences (corporate executives, academic). In these cases:
Reduce to 3 memes (Slots 1, 3, 6 only - Hook, Strategy, Price)
Use subtle Incongruity Theory (intellectual "aha!" moments, not laugh-out-loud)
Increase Visual Hook count (swap memes for additional hooks showing authority/results)
Adjust TTT temperature (likely TTT 1-2 "Diplomatic" or "Professional Advocate")
Check tribe_soul_profile.humor_style - if "minimal" or "professional only," this signals restraint.
