TTT Selection Workflow
Step 1: Analyze target_audience.md
What's their tolerance for confrontation?
Do they use raw language themselves?
Are they seeking comfort or truth?
Step 2: Review backstory.md
What's the coach's natural communication style?
Have they built their brand on raw truth or professional polish?
Step 3: Check Context Premise
What's the Enemies category? (Abstract = lower TTT; Specific = higher TTT)
What are the Emotional Triggers? (Professional = lower; Rage/vindication = higher)
Step 4: Set TTT Level by Module
Introduction Slides: Base TTT or TTT -1 (build trust)
Content Slides: Base TTT (primary temperature)
Transition Slides: Base TTT +1 (create urgency)
Close Slides: Base TTT +1 (action pressure)
TTT Guardrails (Non-Negotiable)
What TTT Does NOT Allow (Any Level):
❌ Attacks on inherent worth: Never "you're stupid," only "that belief is wrong"
❌ Discrimination: No racism, sexism, homophobia (critique ideology, not identity)
❌ False claims: Everything must be researched and backed by data
❌ Belittling trauma: Can challenge victim mindset, never mock real pain
❌ Illegal advice: No matter the TTT level, never encourage harm
The Protective Warrior Test: Before publishing any slide text, ask:
"Am I attacking the PROBLEM or the PERSON?"
"Would this make my audience feel PROTECTED or INSULTED?"
"Is this TRUE and can I back it up?"

📚 JASON FLADLIEN'S 8 REACTION PATTERNS
These patterns should be woven throughout the webinar script:
1. Challenge Core Assumptions
Identify and challenge foundational beliefs
Example: "You think you need a huge audience. That's backwards."
2. Expose Perceived Hypocrisy
Point out contradictions in conventional wisdom
Example: "They tell you to work harder, then sell you rest. Which is it?"
3. Dismiss and Redirect
Acknowledge objection, then redirect to better path
Example: "I hear 'I can't afford it.' Real question: Can you afford not to?"
4. Employ Logical Reversal
Use prospect's logic against their objection
Example: "If you can't afford $7, you definitely can't afford staying stuck."
5. Create Comparative Scenarios
Present two futures (DIY vs. guided)
Example: "Option 1: Struggle alone for months. Option 2: Fast-track with us."
6. Utilize Memorable Metaphors
Use vivid analogies to explain complex concepts
Example: "Your business is like a garden. You can't just plant seeds and hope."
7. Establish Clear Value Hierarchies
Prioritize what truly matters
Example: "Level 1: Make money. Level 2: Make time. Level 3: Make impact."
8. Frame Individual as Societal
Show how one case represents larger trend
Example: "You're not alone. 10,000 people face this exact problem daily."

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: Can I adjust the 72-slide structure?
A: No. The 72-slide framework (12/36/8/16) is based on Jason Fladlien's proven conversion psychology. Deviating from this structure will reduce effectiveness. However, you can adjust content within each module.
Q: Why only 12 visual hooks instead of more?
A: Strategic impact over saturation. Each of the 12 hooks is a major story beat that audiences remember. More hooks create visual fatigue and dilute narrative power. Text slides between hooks maintain pacing, and the addition of 6 memes + 12 real images + 2 reaction videos provides plenty of visual variety without overwhelming the viewer.
Q: What if my audience doesn't fit neatly into a TTT level?
A: Use TTT adjustments by module. Example:
Introduction: TTT 2 (build trust)
Content: TTT 3 (truth-telling)
Transition: TTT 4 (urgency)
Close: TTT 4 (action pressure)
This lets you calibrate temperature throughout the journey.
Q: Do I have to create all 12 real images from scratch?
A: No. Many real images come from existing content:
Screenshots of dashboards, testimonials, program materials
Photos from your personal archive (before/after states)
Simple Canva designs (guarantee badges, CTA buttons)
Client-submitted content (with permission)
Only 3-4 images typically require new photography.
Q: How do I source reaction videos if I don't have client testimonials yet?
A: Options in priority order:
Offer free coaching in exchange for video testimonial
Use licensed social media content (TikTok, Instagram with creator permission)
Record yourself reacting to common struggles/wins
Use stock reaction videos as last resort (least powerful)
Even 1 authentic client testimonial beats 10 stock reactions.
Q: Can I use this for high-ticket offers ($997+)?
A: The current framework is optimized for low-ticket ($7-$47) front-end offers. For high-ticket, extend Module 4 (Close) with:
Additional credibility layers (case studies, media mentions)
Deeper objection handling (fear, skepticism, comparison)
Longer close (20-24 slides instead of 16)
TTT adjustment (usually TTT -1 for high-ticket = more trust-building)
More real images showing transformation depth
Q: What's the difference between VO Script and Slide Text?
A:
VO Script: What the coach SAYS (natural voice, backstory.md tone, authentic, conversational)
Slide Text: What appears ON SCREEN (TTT-calibrated, bold, attention-engineered, tribal language)
Example:
VO Script: "You know, I used to think I needed a huge email list before I could sell anything. Turns out, that's completely backwards."
Slide Text (TTT 3): "Stop Waiting for 10,000 Followers—Start Selling NOW"
Q: How do I know if my TTT level is right?
A: Test these three questions:
"Would my target audience use this language in their own head?"
"Does this feel protective (fighting FOR them) or preachy (talking AT them)?"
"Can I back every claim with data, examples, or lived experience?"
If yes to all three → Correct TTT.
 If no to any → Adjust up or down.
Q: What if I don't have testimonials for proof.md?
A: Use these alternatives:
Your own transformation story (before/after metrics)
Beta test results (even if unpaid)
Aggregate stats ("Tested with 10 people, 7 hit X result")
Case study from ONE successful person
Industry statistics that prove the method works
Credibility can be built without dozens of testimonials. Authenticity > quantity.
Q: How many memes is too few vs. too many?
A: 6 is the sweet spot. Here's why:
Too few (1-3): Misses opportunities for pattern interrupts and viral moments
Just right (6): Strategic placements maintain energy without oversaturation
Too many (12+): Dilutes professionalism, competes with visual hooks, feels gimmicky
Quality > quantity. Each of the 6 memes should be genuinely funny and serve the emotional arc.

        Test accent color on black vs. white, choose higher contrast.
        Default to black if contrast is equal.
        """
        accent = brand_config['colors']['accent']
        # Simplified contrast calculation (full implementation would use WCAG formula)
        return '000000'  # Default to black
    
    def create_slide(self, slide_data):
        """
        Create a single slide with layout, text, and visual assets.
        
        Args:
            slide_data: dict with keys:
                - module: str (Introduction, Content, Transition, Close)
                - slide_number: int
                - title: str
                - slide_text: str (TTT-calibrated)
                - vo_script: str (coach's natural voice)
                - visual_type: str (visual_hook, meme, real_image, reaction_video, text_only)
                - visual_asset: dict (if applicable)
                - speaker_notes: str
                - ttt_temperature: int (1-6)
                - accent_words: list (2-3 words to highlight)
        """
        layout = self._select_layout(slide_data['visual_type'])
        slide = self.prs.slides.add_slide(layout)
        
        # Set background
        self._set_background(slide)
        
        # Add content based on visual type
        if slide_data['visual_type'] == 'visual_hook':
            self._add_visual_hook_slide(slide, slide_data)
        elif slide_data['visual_type'] == 'meme':
            self._add_meme_slide(slide, slide_data)
        elif slide_data['visual_type'] == 'real_image':
            self._add_real_image_slide(slide, slide_data)
        elif slide_data['visual_type'] == 'reaction_video':
            self._add_reaction_video_slide(slide, slide_data)
        elif slide_data['visual_type'] == 'text_only':
            self._add_text_slide(slide, slide_data)
        
        # Add speaker notes (VO script + delivery cues)
        self._add_speaker_notes(slide, slide_data)
        
        return slide
    
    def _set_background(self, slide):
        """Set slide background color (black or white)."""
        background = slide.background
        fill = background.fill
        fill.solid()
        fill.fore_color.rgb = RGBColor.from_string(self.bg_color)
    
    def _add_visual_hook_slide(self, slide, data):
        """
        PAGE A: Bold text top, visual hook image bottom (4:3, 1000x750px)
        - 48px margins all around
        - 48px spacing between text and image
        - 51px corner rounding on image (applied pre-insertion)
        """
        # Top section: Hook text with accent highlights
        text_box = slide.shapes.add_textbox(
            self.MARGIN_STANDARD,  # 48px left
            self.MARGIN_STANDARD,  # 48px top
            self.CANVAS_SIZE - (2 * self.MARGIN_STANDARD),  # Full width minus margins
            Inches(2.5)  # Approximate height for text
        )
        text_frame = text_box.text_frame
        text_frame.word_wrap = True
        
        # Parse hook text for accent highlighting
        hook_text = data['slide_text']  # 7-12 words ideal
        accent_words = data.get('accent_words', [])
        self._apply_hook_text_with_accents(text_frame, hook_text, accent_words)
        
        # Bottom section: Visual hook image (4:3 ratio, 1000x750px)
        # Note: Corner rounding must be applied to image file before insertion
        img_path = data['visual_asset']['file_path']
        img_top = self.MARGIN_STANDARD + Inches(2.5) + self.MARGIN_STANDARD  # Text + spacing
        
        slide.shapes.add_picture(
            img_path,
            self.MARGIN_STANDARD,  # 48px left margin
            img_top,
            self.VISUAL_HOOK_WIDTH,  # 1000px width
            self.VISUAL_HOOK_HEIGHT  # 750px height (4:3)
        )
    
    def _add_meme_slide(self, slide, data):
        """
        PAGE B: Full 1:1 meme image (1080x1080px), no margins
        """
        img_path = data['visual_asset']['file_path']
        slide.shapes.add_picture(
            img_path,
            Inches(0),  # No margin
            Inches(0),  # No margin
            self.MEME_SIZE,  # 1080x1080px, full canvas
            self.MEME_SIZE
        )
    
    def _add_real_image_slide(self, slide, data):
        """
        PAGE D: Optional headline + real image (4:3, 1000x750px)
        """
        # Optional headline
        if data.get('headline'):
            headline_box = slide.shapes.add_textbox(
                self.MARGIN_STANDARD,
                self.MARGIN_STANDARD,
                self.CANVAS_SIZE - (2 * self.MARGIN_STANDARD),
                Inches(1.5)
            )
            headline_frame = headline_box.text_frame
            headline_frame.text = data['headline']
            headline_frame.paragraphs[0].font.size = Pt(42)
            headline_frame.paragraphs[0].font.bold = True
            headline_frame.paragraphs[0].font.name = 'Open Sans'
            headline_frame.paragraphs[0].font.color.rgb = RGBColor.from_string(self.text_color)
            
            img_top = self.MARGIN_STANDARD + Inches(1.5) + self.MARGIN_STANDARD
        else:
            img_top = self.MARGIN_STANDARD
        
        # Real image (corner rounding applied pre-insertion)
        img_path = data['visual_asset']['file_path']
        slide.shapes.add_picture(
            img_path,
            self.MARGIN_STANDARD,
            img_top,
            self.REAL_IMAGE_WIDTH,  # 1000px width
            self.REAL_IMAGE_HEIGHT  # 750px height (4:3)
        )
    
    def _add_reaction_video_slide(self, slide, data):
        """
        PAGE C: Context text top, reaction video bottom (4:3, 1000x750px)
        """
        # Context text
        text_box = slide.shapes.add_textbox(
            self.MARGIN_STANDARD,
            self.MARGIN_STANDARD,
            self.CANVAS_SIZE - (2 * self.MARGIN_STANDARD),
            Inches(1.5)
        )
        text_frame = text_box.text_frame
        text_frame.text = data['context_text']
        text_frame.paragraphs[0].font.size = Pt(32)
        text_frame.paragraphs[0].font.name = 'Open Sans'
        text_frame.paragraphs[0].font.color.rgb = RGBColor.from_string(self.text_color)
        
        # Reaction video (corner rounding applied pre-insertion)
        video_path = data['visual_asset']['file_path']
        video_top = self.MARGIN_STANDARD + Inches(1.5) + self.MARGIN_STANDARD
        
        slide.shapes.add_movie(
            video_path,
            self.MARGIN_STANDARD,
            video_top,
            self.VISUAL_HOOK_WIDTH,  # 1000px width
            self.VISUAL_HOOK_HEIGHT  # 750px height (4:3)
        )
    
    def _add_text_slide(self, slide, data):
        """
        PAGE E: Headline + bullet points OR
        PAGE F: Centered quote with emoji
        """
        if data.get('slide_format') == 'quote':
            # PAGE F: Centered quote
            quote_box = slide.shapes.add_textbox(
                self.MARGIN_STANDARD * 2,
                self.CANVAS_SIZE / 2 - Inches(2),
                self.CANVAS_SIZE - (4 * self.MARGIN_STANDARD),
                Inches(4)
            )
            quote_frame = quote_box.text_frame
            quote_frame.text = data['slide_text']
            quote_frame.paragraphs[0].alignment = PP_ALIGN.CENTER
            quote_frame.paragraphs[0].font.size = Pt(42)
            quote_frame.paragraphs[0].font.bold = True
            quote_frame.paragraphs[0].font.name = 'Open Sans'
            quote_frame.paragraphs[0].font.color.rgb = RGBColor.from_string(self.text_color)
            
            # Apply accent to key words
            if data.get('accent_words'):
                self._apply_hook_text_with_accents(quote_frame, data['slide_text'], data['accent_words'])
        
        else:
            # PAGE E: Headline + bullets
            # Headline
            headline_box = slide.shapes.add_textbox(
                self.MARGIN_STANDARD,
                self.MARGIN_STANDARD,
                self.CANVAS_SIZE - (2 * self.MARGIN_STANDARD),
                Inches(1.5)
            )
            headline_frame = headline_box.text_frame
            headline_frame.text = data['headline']
            headline_frame.paragraphs[0].font.size = Pt(42)
            headline_frame.paragraphs[0].font.bold = True
            headline_frame.paragraphs[0].font.name = 'Open Sans'
            headline_frame.paragraphs[0].font.color.rgb = RGBColor.from_string(self.text_color)
            
            # Bullets
            if data.get('bullets'):
                bullets_box = slide.shapes.add_textbox(
                    self.MARGIN_STANDARD,
                    self.MARGIN_STANDARD + Inches(2),
                    self.CANVAS_SIZE - (2 * self.MARGIN_STANDARD),
                    Inches(6)
                )
                bullets_frame = bullets_box.text_frame
                
                for bullet in data['bullets']:
                    p = bullets_frame.add_paragraph()
                    p.text = bullet
                    p.level = 0
                    p.font.size = Pt(28)
                    p.font.name = 'Open Sans'
                    p.font.color.rgb = RGBColor.from_string(self.text_color)
    
    def _apply_hook_text_with_accents(self, text_frame, hook_text, accent_words):
        """
        Apply hook text with 2-3 words highlighted in accent color.
        Hook text should be 7-12 words (max 15).
        """
        paragraph = text_frame.paragraphs[0]
        paragraph.clear()
        words = hook_text.split()
        
        # Determine font size based on word count
        word_count = len(words)
        if word_count <= 8:
            font_size = Pt(48)
        elif word_count <= 11:
            font_size = Pt(42)
        else:
            font_size = Pt(36)
        
        for word in words:
            run = paragraph.add_run()
            run.text = word + ' '
            run.font.size = font_size
            run.font.bold = True
            run.font.name = 'Open Sans'
            
            # Highlight accent words (2-3 emotional triggers)
            clean_word = word.strip('.,!?').lower()
            if any(accent.lower() == clean_word for accent in accent_words):
                run.font.color.rgb = RGBColor.from_string(self.brand['colors']['accent'])
            else:
                run.font.color.rgb = RGBColor.from_string(self.text_color)
    
    def _add_speaker_notes(self, slide, data):
        """Add formatted speaker notes with VO script + delivery cues."""
        notes_slide = slide.notes_slide
        notes_text = f"""
VO SCRIPT (Coach's Natural Voice):
{data['vo_script']}

DELIVERY NOTES:
- Display Time: {data.get('display_time', '6-8 seconds')}
- Sound Effect: {data.get('sound_effect', 'None')}
- Coach Video: {data.get('coach_video_notes', 'Standard position')}
- Energy Level: {data.get('energy_level', 'Moderate')}
- TTT Temperature: Level {data.get('ttt_temperature', self.ttt_level)}
        """
        notes_slide.notes_text_frame.text = notes_text
    
    def save(self, output_path):
        """Save presentation to file."""
        self.prs.save(output_path)
        print(f"✅ Presentation saved to {output_path}")
