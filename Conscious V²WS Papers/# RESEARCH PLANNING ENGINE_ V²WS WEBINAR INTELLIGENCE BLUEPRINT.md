## 7-DIMENSIONAL RESEARCH PLANNING FRAMEWORK

### Planning Dimension 1: Historical Evolution & Transformation Timeline

**Strategic Focus:** Map "then vs. now" storytelling opportunities for Introduction hooks and Content context

**Investigation Targets:**

- Historical timeline of [Topic] evolution over past 20-50 years
- Methodology shifts and approach changes over time
- Statistical contrasts showing dramatic changes (then vs. now data)
- Cultural norm reversals relevant to [Target Audience]
- Generational wisdom gaps and outdated advice still circulating
- Key turning points that changed the landscape

**Module Applications:**

- **Introduction:** Historical context for "why old ways don't work"
- **Content:** Context phase showing evolution of understanding
- **Transition:** Contrast DIY (old way) vs. guided (new way)

**Research Questions to Answer:**

1. What did people believe about [Topic] 20 years ago that's now proven wrong?
2. How has the problem [Target Audience] faces changed over time?
3. What systemic shifts created new challenges or opportunities?

---

### Planning Dimension 2: Contrarian Analysis & Sacred Cow Demolition

**Strategic Focus:** Surface pattern interruption opportunities for Introduction hooks and objection pre-framing

**Investigation Targets:**

- Mainstream narratives currently dominating [Topic] conversation
- Expert contrarian voices challenging conventional wisdom
- Industry insider knowledge contradicting public messaging
- Suppressed downsides of popular approaches
- Myths [Target Audience] holds that research contradicts
- Hidden agendas or conflicts of interest in mainstream advice

**Module Applications:**

- **Introduction:** Outrageous truth bombs and "wait, what?" moments
- **Content:** Strategy phase showing counterintuitive approaches
- **Close:** Objection handling with contrarian evidence

**Research Questions to Answer:**

1. What is everyone told about [Topic] that's actually wrong?
2. Which "experts" are saying the opposite of mainstream advice?
3. What uncomfortable truths is the industry hiding from [Target Audience]?

---

### Planning Dimension 3: Emotional Landscape & Human Struggle Documentation

**Strategic Focus:** Capture raw human experience for Introduction hope creation and Content vision phases

**Investigation Targets:**

- [Target Audience] pain point documentation from social media, forums, Reddit
- Success story turning points and breakthrough moments
- Visceral metaphors and language [Target Audience] uses to describe struggles
- Community insider phrases and shared references
- Unfiltered emotional expressions (frustration, desperation, hope, relief)
- "Rock bottom" moments and catalysts for change

**Module Applications:**

- **Introduction:** Micro-commitment and relatability creation
- **Content:** Vision phase showing transformation possibility
- **Transition:** Choice framework emotional weight

**Research Questions to Answer:**

1. How does [Target Audience] actually talk about their struggles with [Topic]?
2. What metaphors do they use to describe their pain?
3. What were the "final straw" moments that made them seek solutions?

---

### Planning Dimension 4: Data-Driven Reality & Statistical Shock Value

**Strategic Focus:** Quantify human experience with surprising statistics for Introduction authority and Close urgency

**Investigation Targets:**

- Success/failure rates for common approaches to [Topic]
- Demographic breakdowns specific to [Target Audience]
- Surprise statistics that contradict intuition or common belief
- Hidden costs (time, money, health, relationships) of status quo
- Actual odds of desired outcomes with and without intervention
- ROI data and transformation timelines with supporting evidence

**Module Applications:**

- **Introduction:** Shocking statistics for hook and authority
- **Content:** Strategy phase proof points
- **Close:** Value stack quantification and cost of inaction

**Research Questions to Answer:**

1. What shocking statistic about [Topic] would make [Target Audience] stop scrolling?
2. What's the actual cost (quantified) of NOT solving this problem?
3. What success rates can be proven with [Coach's Approach] vs. alternatives?

---

### Planning Dimension 5: Cultural Zeitgeist & Current Moment Relevance

**Strategic Focus:** Capture "happening NOW" urgency for Introduction hooks and Transition timing justification

**Investigation Targets:**

- Recent viral moments related to [Topic] (last 6-12 months)
- Technology disruptions affecting [Topic] or [Target Audience]
- Economic factors creating new urgency (last 12 months)
- Generational shifts in approach to [Topic]
- Trending conversations on social platforms (Twitter, TikTok, Reddit, LinkedIn)
- Recent news events or studies creating cultural awareness

**Module Applications:**

- **Introduction:** "Why now?" urgency creation
- **Content:** Context phase showing current landscape
- **Transition:** Timing justification for taking action
- **Close:** FOMO and urgency amplification

**Research Questions to Answer:**

1. What happened in the last 6 months that makes [Topic] more urgent now?
2. What are people currently saying about [Topic] on social media?
3. What trending moment can we reference for instant relatability?

---

### Planning Dimension 6: Cross-Disciplinary Frameworks & Metaphorical Models

**Strategic Focus:** Generate novel explanatory frameworks for Content strategy phases and memorable teaching

**Investigation Targets:**

- [Topic] explained through biological/evolutionary lens
- [Topic] explained through military/strategic lens
- [Topic] explained through physics/systems thinking lens
- [Topic] explained through psychological/behavioral science lens
- Successful frameworks from other industries applicable to [Topic]
- Metaphors that resonate with [Target Audience] worldview and values

**Module Applications:**

- **Content:** Strategy phase teaching using memorable metaphors
- **Introduction:** Authority building through unique perspective
- **Transition:** Simplification of complex concepts for confidence

**Research Questions to Answer:**

1. What metaphor from another field perfectly explains [Topic]?
2. How would [relevant discipline] approach solving this problem?
3. What framework makes complex [Topic] concepts instantly understandable?

---

### Planning Dimension 7: Objection Intelligence & Resistance Dissolution

**Strategic Focus:** Pre-identify and neutralize objections for Introduction pre-framing and Close objection handling

**Investigation Targets:**

- Common objections [Target Audience] has about [Topic] solutions
- Time, money, and capability objection evidence and counter-evidence
- Skepticism patterns and what creates belief shifts
- Social proof elements that overcome specific objections
- Expert validation addressing [Target Audience] specific doubts
- "I tried this before" failure pattern analysis and differentiation proof

**Module Applications:**

- **Introduction:** Objection pre-frame planting early seeds
- **Content:** Commitment phases building confidence
- **Transition:** Two choices framework addressing DIY concerns
- **Close:** Full objection destruction sequence

**Research Questions to Answer:**

1. What objections will [Target Audience] have before we even present the offer?
2. What evidence dissolves each major objection category?
3. What makes someone with past failures believe THIS time will be different?
