🌡️ TEMPERAMENT TEMPERATURE TONE (TTT) SYSTEM
Version 1.0 - Slide Text & Visual Hook Copy Calibration Tool

System Purpose
The TTT System is a 6-level temperature dial that controls the rawness, directness, and emotional charge of:
Slide Text (headlines, bullet points, on-screen copy)
Visual Hook Copy (meme text, pain visualizations, choice framework language)
Call-to-Action phrasing (buttons, CTAs, urgency language)
CRITICAL DISTINCTION:
VO Script = Coach's natural voice (separate calibration based on their backstory.md)
Slide Text = Attention engineering (TTT calibration)
Visual Hooks = Pattern interrupt + emotional resonance (TTT calibration)
Why This Matters: People's internal dialogue is raw, uncensored, and character-specific. If your slide text doesn't match their internal voice, they'll feel the disconnect and disengage.

The 6 TTT Temperature Levels
Level 1: DIPLOMATIC (10°F)
Use Case: Corporate coaches, HR training, leadership development, academia
 Audience Tolerance: Very low tolerance for controversy; high need for professionalism
 Language Profile: Neutral, professional, abstracted, no named enemies
Characteristics:
Zero profanity (not even "damn" or "hell")
No personal attacks (ever)
Vague opponents: "outdated systems," "traditional models," "limiting beliefs"
Passive voice preferred: "Mistakes were made" vs. "They lied to you"
Euphemisms: "challenging," "suboptimal," "requires refinement"
No hero/villain dynamics: Collaborative framing only
Example Slide Text:
Before (Raw): "Stop letting toxic bosses steal your best years"
 Level 1 Version: "Evolve Beyond Limiting Leadership Dynamics"
Before (Raw): "The education system failed you—here's why"
 Level 1 Version: "Reimagining Professional Development Pathways"
Context Premise Application:
Enemies: "Outdated paradigms," "systemic inefficiencies"
Emotional Triggers: Avoided entirely; focus on "opportunity" language
Success Markers: Professional language ("executive presence," "strategic influence")

Level 2: PROFESSIONAL ADVOCATE (30°F)
Use Case: Business coaching, career pivots, personal finance, wellness
 Audience Tolerance: Moderate; appreciates directness but needs polish
 Language Profile: Assertive but professional, clear opponents (systems, not people)
Characteristics:
Rare profanity: Only "damn" or "hell" for emphasis (1-2x per webinar)
System critique allowed: Can attack industries, institutions, not individuals
Opponents named broadly: "The 9-5 trap," "diet culture," "hustle culture"
Active voice: "You deserve better" (direct address)
Mild confrontation: "Stop settling" (but not "you're stupid for settling")
Hero/helper dynamics: Coach as guide, audience as emerging hero
Example Slide Text:
Before (Raw): "F*ck the 9-5—build your empire"
 Level 2 Version: "Break Free from the 9-5 Trap—Here's How"
Before (Raw): "Your boss is gaslighting you into staying broke"
 Level 2 Version: "How Corporate Culture Keeps You Underpaid (And What to Do About It)"
Context Premise Application:
Enemies: "The corporate ladder," "diet industry lies," "traditional finance gatekeepers"
Emotional Triggers: "trapped," "underpaid," "overlooked"
Success Markers: "6-figure income," "time freedom," "financial independence"

Level 3: TRUTH-TELLER (50°F)
Use Case: Relationship coaching, mindset, entrepreneurship, fitness
 Audience Tolerance: High; wants honesty even if uncomfortable
 Language Profile: Direct, no-BS, challenges assumptions, named opponents (ideologies)
Characteristics:
Strategic profanity: "BS," "crap," occasional "shit" (5-7x per webinar)
Ideology critique: Can attack belief systems ("toxic positivity," "feminist dating advice")
Opponents named specifically: "Instagram gurus," "mainstream media," "your broke friends"
Confrontational questions: "Why are you lying to yourself?"
Hero/villain clear: Audience = hero held back by external lies + internal excuses
Tough love allowed: "You're capable of more—stop playing small"
Example Slide Text:
Before (Raw): "These f*cking influencers are lying to you"
 Level 3 Version: "Instagram Gurus Are Selling You BS—Here's the Real Path"
Before (Raw): "Your friends are broke losers holding you back"
 Level 3 Version: "Real Talk: Your Circle Is Keeping You Stuck (Here's Why)"
Context Premise Application:
Enemies: "Fake gurus," "your comfort zone," "society's lies about success"
Emotional Triggers: "fed up," "done settling," "tired of fake people"
Success Markers: "first $10K month," "quit my job," "became the example"

Level 4: PROTECTIVE WARRIOR (70°F)
Use Case: Men's coaching, high-performance, wealth building, transformation
 Audience Tolerance: Very high; craves unfiltered truth and protective advocacy
 Language Profile: Raw, polarizing, fiercely protective, names individuals + institutions
Characteristics:
Full profanity arsenal: "F*ck," "bullshit," "damn," "hell" (10-15x per webinar)
Named enemies: Specific people, companies, ideologies ("Feminist dating coaches," "Wall Street," "Big Pharma")
Aggressive protection: "I'll be damned if I let them destroy you"
Benign violations: Shocking language paired with protective intent
Hero/villain/helper: Clear triadic storytelling (you = hero, enemy = named, coach = helper/warrior)
No apologies: "This will offend some people. Good."
Example Slide Text:
Before (Conservative): "Challenge limiting beliefs about money"
 Level 4 Version: "Stop Letting Broke People Tell You Money Is Evil"
Before (Conservative): "Overcome self-doubt in relationships"
 Level 4 Version: "She's Not 'Out of Your League'—Society Lied to You"
Context Premise Application:
Enemies: "Woke culture," "your ex who gaslit you," "the system designed to keep you weak"
Emotional Triggers: "rage at injustice," "protective fury," "vindication"
Success Markers: "6-figure business," "walked away from toxic relationship," "built empire"

Level 5: RAW CONFRONTATION (85°F)
Use Case: Red pill content, controversial niches, rage-bait growth strategy
 Audience Tolerance: Extremely high; seeks controversy and polarization
 Language Profile: Provocative, divisive, unapologetic, intentionally triggering
Characteristics:
Unrestricted profanity: All language allowed, frequent use
Named individuals: Can reference specific public figures or viral moments
Intentional polarization: Designed to create strong agree/disagree split
Aggressive callouts: "If you believe X, you're part of the problem"
Victim/victor framing: "Stop being a victim—become dangerous"
No softening: Zero concern for offending; offense is strategy
Example Slide Text:
Before (Conservative): "Improve your dating life"
 Level 5 Version: "Why Modern Women Aren't Worth Your Time (And What to Do Instead)"
Before (Conservative): "Build financial confidence"
 Level 5 Version: "Society Wants You Broke and Obedient—Here's How to Fight Back"
Context Premise Application:
Enemies: "Feminists," "the woke mob," "weak men," "entitled women"
Emotional Triggers: "rage," "vindication," "us vs. them," "reclaim power"
Success Markers: "I stopped caring what people think," "I triggered the right people"

Level 6: UNFILTERED TRUTH BOMB (100°F)
Use Case: Highly controversial, cult-following niches, maximum polarization
 Audience Tolerance: Only for those seeking pure unfiltered reality
 Language Profile: Zero filters, maximum shock value, cultural third rail topics
Characteristics:
No language restrictions: Full cursing, slang, street language
Cultural third rails: Willing to touch topics others avoid (gender dynamics, race, politics, religion)
Maximum villain specificity: Names names, cites examples, no abstractions
Aggressive framing: "If this offends you, leave now"
Cult-building language: "Only the strong survive this," "We are the 1%"
Total tribal identity: Clear in-group/out-group language
Example Slide Text:
Before (Conservative): "Navigate modern relationships"
 Level 6 Version: "The Truth About Body Count No One Will Tell You (Prepare to Be Triggered)"
Before (Conservative): "Achieve financial success"
 Level 6 Version: "How the Government & Banks Conspire to Keep You Poor (Receipts Included)"
Context Premise Application:
Enemies: Specific ideologies, movements, public figures (named)
Emotional Triggers: "forbidden truth," "they don't want you to know," "cancel-worthy"
Success Markers: "I said what everyone's thinking," "I don't care who I offend"

Implementation Guidelines
Selecting TTT Level
Step 1: Analyze target_audience.md
What's their tolerance for confrontation?
Do they use raw language themselves?
Are they seeking comfort or truth?
Step 2: Review backstory.md
What's the coach's natural communication style?
Have they built their brand on raw truth or professional polish?
Step 3: Check Context Premise
What's the Enemies category? (Abstract = lower TTT; Specific = higher TTT)
What are the Emotional Triggers? (Professional = lower; Rage/vindication = higher)
Step 4: Set TTT Level for:
Introduction Slides (usually TTT -1 from content)
Content Slides (primary TTT level)
Transition Slides (usually TTT +1 for urgency)
Close Slides (usually TTT +1 for action)

TTT Guardrails
What TTT Does NOT Allow (Any Level):
Attacks on inherent worth: Never "you're stupid," only "that belief is wrong"
Discrimination: No racism, sexism, homophobia (critique ideology, not identity)
False claims: Everything must be researched and backed by data
Belittling trauma: Can challenge victim mindset, never mock real pain
Illegal advice: No matter the TTT level, never encourage harm
The Protective Warrior Test:
Before publishing any slide text, ask:
"Am I attacking the PROBLEM or the PERSON?"
"Would this make my audience feel PROTECTED or INSULTED?"
"Is this TRUE and can I back it up?"

Final Note: TTT Is a Tool, Not a Mandate
The Goal: Match the audience's internal emotional reality, not shock for shock's sake.
The Test: After generating slide text, ask:
"Would my target audience nod and say 'finally, someone said it'?"
"Does this make them feel seen, validated, and protected?"
"Can I back every claim with data, examples, or lived experience?"
If yes to all three → Publish.
 If no to any → Recalibrate TTT or rewrite.
