# **Product Requirements Document (PRD)**

## **1\. Goals and Background Context**

### **1.1 The Strategic Vision: Identity Engineering**

The Conscious Behavioral Change System represents a categorical paradigm shift in the personal development industry. We are formally abandoning the "Habit Tracker" model—characterized by passive logging, graphical dashboards, and administrative friction—in favor of an **"Identity Engineering"** model.

Our vision is to deploy a **Conversational Operating System** that lives entirely within the user's existing digital environment: **Telegram**. By removing the graphical user interface (GUI) and replacing it with a sophisticated **Agent-Orchestrated** backend, we address the primary failure mode of the 50-65 demographic: **Compliance Fatigue**. We are not building a tool for users to manage; we are building a cybernetic loop for users to trust. The system functions as an "External Pre-Frontal Cortex," automating the psychological heavy lifting required to bridge the gap between who a user is today and who they wish to become.

This system operates on the principle of **Self-Perception Cybernetics**. We do not try to change behavior through willpower or motivation, both of which are depletable resources. Instead, we change behavior by altering the user's self-concept through the relentless injection of high-fidelity evidence. By guiding the user to perform micro-actions and capturing undeniable proof of those actions, the system "denoises" the user's identity, allowing them to infer a new sense of self from their own observed behavior.

### **1.2 Core Goals**

#### **1.2.1 For the Coach (B2B): The Master Composer**

The primary B2B goal is to resolve the "Healer’s Dilemma"—the inherent conflict between maintaining high-touch emotional intimacy and achieving financial scale. We are moving away from "Course Building" (creating linear, static content timelines) to **"Dynamic Assembly"** (managing modular, adaptive ingredients).

* **Operational Goal:** To empower the coach to act as a "Master Composer" who manages a **"Component Pantry"** of transformational ingredients (Rituals), rather than an administrator managing user lists.  
* **Scaling Goal:** To allow a single coach to serve 1,000+ clients with hyper-personalized daily protocols. This is achieved by **Atlas (The Program Architect)**, an AI agent that automatically constructs bespoke programs based on user assessment data and **Context Premise** mapping. The system dynamically selects "Lego Blocks" of content and wraps them in specific persuasion logic, requiring zero manual intervention from the coach for daily delivery.  
* **Insight Goal:** To replace "Data Overload" with "Intuitive Signal." The Coach Dashboard visualizes the aggregate **"Cohort Vibe"** (via NLP-driven Word Clouds generated by **Maeva**) and specific **"Red Flag"** risks, allowing the coach to understand the psychological state of the group in seconds and intervene with precision via **Operator Mode**.

#### **1.2.2 For the Client (B2C): The Evidence Printer**

The primary B2C goal is to resolve **Cognitive Dissonance**—the mental stress caused when a user's High Goals (e.g., "I want vitality") conflict with their Current Identity (e.g., "I am exhausted").

* **Experience Goal:** Deliver a **"Zero-UI"** experience. The client never downloads an app, resets a password, or navigates a menu. They simply converse with an intelligent Agent in Telegram.  
* **Psychological Goal:** To shift the user's identity from the outside in. By using the **"Vision Implant"** technique (Morning) to prime action, and the **"Evidence Capture"** loop (Evening) to validate success, the system generates undeniable proof of change. This "prints" the new identity into the user's mind.  
* **Engagement Goal:** Achieve \>80% daily interaction rates by leveraging the **"Mirroring Effect."** The Agent uses high-fidelity voice synthesis via **IndexTTS-2** to speak to the user, compelling them to reply with voice. This captures rich, unstructured "Soul Data" that text-based apps miss.

#### **1.2.3 For the Business: Sustainable Unit Economics**

The goal is to make high-touch AI economically viable. We implement a robust **Stripe Connect** infrastructure that automatically splits revenue ($95 Coach / $5 Platform) to cover the high marginal costs of Neuro-Persuasion (GPU inference \+ LLM tokens). We enforce a **"Cost Circuit Breaker"** via **Langfuse** monitoring to ensure no single "Power User" erodes the platform's margin by generating excessive audio without corresponding value.

### **1.3 Background Context & Technical Philosophy**

#### **1.3.1 The Intelligence Architecture: The Agentic Team**

To execute this delicate psychological operation at scale, standard chatbots are insufficient. We require a deterministic **Agent-Orchestrated System**. We do not treat the AI as a "Magic Box"; we treat it as a staff of specialized experts.

* **Emilio (The Orchestrator):** Manages the user's state in **LangGraph**, ensuring the conversation flows logically from "Priming" to "Action" to "Reflection."  
* **Aria (The Synthesizer):** Listens to the user's raw voice notes (transcribed by **Groq**) and performs entity extraction. She does not reply; she listens, mapping the user's "Enemies," "Fears," and "Dreams" into the **Neo4j** graph.  
* **The Assembler (The Strategist):** Consults the **Intelligence Library** to select the correct psychological lever (e.g., "The Challenger") based on the user's current state.  
* **The Artisan (The Copywriter):** Generates the script using **MiniMax-M2**, strictly adhering to the syntax rules of the selected TTT (Temperament, Temperature, Tone) voice.

#### **1.3.2 The Intelligence Library (The Logic Kernel)**

The system's psychology is not hard-coded in Python; it is defined in a repository of static YAML configuration files known as the **Intelligence Library**. This includes:

* **identity\_pillars.yaml:** The definitions of the 7 archetypes (Rebel, Maker, Vessel, etc.).  
* **persuasion\_layers.yaml:** The logic structures for influence (e.g., Reverse Psychology, Social Proof).  
* **ttt\_matrix.yaml:** The physics of the voice (Prosody, Breathiness, Speed).

By injecting these files into the agents at runtime via **Pydantic AI**, we ensure that the system remains deterministic and aligned with the coach's methodology, preventing hallucination and ensuring safety.

#### **1.3.3 The Memory Architecture: Mapping "Soul Data"**

Standard apps fail because they rely on shallow demographics ("Age: 55", "Goal: Weight Loss"). This system succeeds because it maps the user's **Context Premise**. We utilize a **Hybrid Memory Architecture**:

* **Supabase (Relational):** Stores the "Hard Logs" (Did they do it? When?).  
* **Neo4j (Graph):** Stores the "Soft Data" (Why didn't they do it?). This graph database allows **The Assembler** to query complex relationships (e.g., *"Find the Enemy node connected to this User that is blocking their Dream node"*), enabling hyper-personalized scripts that feel deeply human.
