# H11: Raw Target Audience Research — Implementation Architecture

**Hypothesis:** Before the Soul Tribe Profile (H9) can be built with real depth, the pipeline needs raw intelligence about the coach's actual target audience — who they are, what they experience, what visual codes they recognize, what language they use, what keeps them awake at 2am.

**Pipeline Position:** CCF Setup Phase → feeds H9 (Soul Tribe Profiles), H1 (Blueprint), H3 (SoC Voice)  
**Existing Infrastructure:** `Deep Target Audience Research Planner` (exists) — but no corresponding analyst to process its output  
**Gap Classification:** HIGH — Planner exists, analyst absent, raw data never processed

## Section 2: The 4 Laws of Audience Research Distillation

### Law 1 — Lived Reality Over Demographics

Demographics (age, location, income) describe a population. They do not describe a tribe. The law requires that every extracted insight pass the "2am test": **Does this describe something the audience actually experiences at 2am when no one is watching?**

- PASS: "She lies awake calculating whether she can afford both the naturopath appointment and her daughter's school supplies."
- FAIL: "Women aged 35-50 in urban France with above-average interest in holistic health."

**Every audience insight must be experiential, not categorical.** The output should read like an ethnographic field report, not a marketing demographic slide.

### Law 2 — Pain/Desire Depth Stratification

Every pain point and desire must be classified:

- **L1 — Stated pain/desire:** What the audience says they want in public. Example: "I want to eat healthier."
- **L2 — Real pain/desire:** What they actually struggle with when they try. Example: "I want to eat healthier but my family sabotages every change I make."
- **L3 — Hidden pain/desire:** What they won't say out loud but feel deeply. Example: "I resent my mother for teaching me food habits that are now destroying my health."

**Depth distribution minimum:** ≥ 30% L2, ≥ 10% L3. If the research only captures L1 pains, the downstream content will be generic motivation that the audience has heard a thousand times.

### Law 3 — Tribal Language Extraction

The audience has its own language. Not the coach's language — the tribe's. The law requires extraction of:

- **In-group vocabulary:** Words and phrases the tribe uses with each other. Terms of recognition. Code words that signal belonging.
- **Rejection vocabulary:** Words and phrases that signal outsider status. Marketing language that makes them scroll past. Buzzwords they associate with inauthenticity.
- **Cultural references:** Specific people, events, media, memes, traditions, and objects that the tribe references. These are the recognition codes that H9 will use for depth profiling.

### Law 4 — Audience Authenticity Gate

1. **Experiential verification:** Every insight must trace to a verifiable source (research finding, forum post, interview quote, trend data). No inferred audience behaviors.
2. **Depth distribution:** ≥ 30% L2 pains/desires, ≥ 10% L3. Below threshold → SHALLOW.
3. **Language validation:** ≥ 10 in-group vocabulary items, ≥ 5 rejection vocabulary items. Below threshold → the research hasn't gone deep enough into actual tribe spaces.
4. **2am test coverage:** ≥ 5 insights that pass the 2am test. These are the insights that will generate RECOGNITION mode content.

## Section 3: Output Format

```
raw_target_audience_research_v{N}.md

├── METADATA { coach, version, date, sources_count }
│
├── TRIBE PORTRAIT
│   ├── Identity markers (who they ARE, not demographics)
│   ├── Daily reality (what a typical day contains)
│   └── 2am insights (what haunts them when alone)
│
├── PAIN/DESIRE MAP (depth-stratified)
│   ├── L1 — Stated (public, safe, shareable)
│   ├── L2 — Real (private, struggled with, rarely admitted)
│   └── L3 — Hidden (unspoken, shame-adjacent, deeply felt)
│
├── TRIBAL LANGUAGE
│   ├── In-group vocabulary [{ term, context, frequency }]
│   ├── Rejection vocabulary [{ term, why_rejected }]
│   └── Cultural references [{ reference, recognition_level, context }]
│
├── VISUAL RECOGNITION CODES
│   ├── Objects/scenes tribe would recognize instantly
│   ├── Aesthetics tribe gravitates toward (colors, styling, environments)
│   └── Visual rejection triggers (what feels "tourist" vs. "insider")
│
└── RESEARCH PROVENANCE
    └── [{ insight_id, source_url, verification_date }]
```

## Section 4: 5 Micro-Hypothesis Evaluations

**MH1 — 2am Test:** Select 5 audience insights. Each must describe an experience that could happen at 2am alone. If < 3 pass, the research is surface-level.

**MH2 — Depth Distribution:** Count pain/desire items per layer. L2 < 30% or L3 < 10% → FAIL.

**MH3 — Language Extraction:** Count in-group vocabulary items (minimum 10) and rejection vocabulary items (minimum 5).

**MH4 — Provenance Test:** Select 5 insights. Each must trace to a specific research source with URL/reference.

**MH5 — Downstream H9 Utility:** Feed the audience research to the Soul Tribe Profile builder (H9). Does the resulting tribe profile contain depth-stratified entries with real recognition codes, or does it default to generic tribe descriptions?
