## OPEN-SOURCE ADVANTAGE: WHY THIS EXISTS

Closed models (DALL-E, Midjourney, Gemini) actively suppress:
- Gender-specific body language (masculine jaw, feminine curve)
- Suggestive but SFW compositions (skin, proximity, tension)
- Raw emotional extremes (real grief, real rage, real ecstasy)
- Editorial fashion posing (vogue, provocative angles)
- Any body language with sexual undertones (even professional advertising-grade)

**Our ControlNet pipeline bypasses this entirely.** iClone poses → ControlNet conditioning → FLUX open-source. No content filter on the COMPOSITION. The identity LoRA brings the coach's face; the pose brings the FULL spectrum of human expression.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Unique expressions | 16 × 6 intensities = 96 | 28 × 6 intensities = 168 |

| MetaHuman render batch | 17,280 | ~30,000 |

| Expression coverage | Professional/Teaching | **Full human emotional spectrum** |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The 12 new channels increase MetaHuman render time from ~24h to ~40h on a single A100, but the expression space goes from "Educator" to "every human emotion a coach could ever need to communicate." The render is a one-time cost.
