## Executive Summary

The FR10-FR19 architecture defines the core logic engines of the CCP: Trigger Matching, Activation Seed formulation, the CRAL 9-Skill Research Engine, and the Final Builder Compilation routing parameters. While the psychological depth (especially in FR18's Zillmann/Higgins mapping and FR14's Diagonal Research structure) is mathematically rigorous, the specs suffer from **severe boundary bleeding** and **contradictory quality gates**. 

Specifically, FR10 accidentally includes the entire job descriptions of FR11 and FR12, while the CRAL quality gates (FR16) physically break the research methodology established in FR14.

## CRITICAL ISSUES (Must be resolved before commit)

### 1. Massive Scope Overlap & Boundary Bleed (FR10 vs FR11 & FR12)
* **Conflict:** FR10 (Four-Axis Structural Matching Engine) is designed to evaluate congruence and output match scores (`DEP-ENG-010`). However, inside the FR10 spec, **Phase 4** explicitly takes over the job of constructing the DARN-CAT Activation Event Seeds (which is the entirety of **FR11**), and **Phase 5** explicitly defines and builds the "Three Failure Prevention Gates" (which is the entirety of **FR12**). 
* **Impact:** The system currently has two sets of conflicting instructions for building seeds and evaluating gates. This will cause catastrophic duplications and pipeline races. 
* **Required Resolution:** FR10 must be surgically amputated at Phase 3 (Match). FR10 should ONLY emit `DEP-ENG-010` (Four-Axis Match Object). Phase 4 and Phase 5 must be entirely deleted from FR10, officially delegating that work to FR11 and FR12.

### 2. Evidence Bias Gate Contradicts CRAL Design Constraints (FR16 vs FR14)
* **Conflict:** FR14 defines 7 specific research moments (M1-M7). M3 (Behavioral Science) and M6 (Investigative Journalism) are explicitly designed to find scientific studies and institutional evidence. However, **FR16 (Human Evidence Bias Gate)** enforces a strict, universal rule that *every single CRAL finding* must be anchored by 3 named individuals, and it explicitly rejects "Experts" (AC2).
* **Impact:** FR16's strict gate mathematically breaks M3 (which requires psychological experts) and M6 (which requires institutional documents, not 3 individual experiencers). If FR16 remains universal, CRAL will perpetually fail at M3 and M6 due to missing "ordinary people" in scientific literature.
* **Required Resolution:** FR16's "Expert Rejection" and "3-Person" rules must be scoped to apply ONLY to Narrative/Experiential CRAL moments (M2, M4, M7). M3, M5, and M6 must have bypass logic allowing expert/institutional evidence validation.

### 3. Colliding Definitions mappings for DEP-ENG-006 (FR10 vs FR13)
* **Conflict:** FR10 imports `DEP-ENG-006` as the standing "Context Premise Map" generated by FR9 (Audience Empathy Agent) for the macro *Audience*. However, FR13 explicitly claims authorship of `DEP-ENG-006`, defining it as the "Client Context Premise Map"—a Neo4j Hypergraph built from one-on-one private telemetry (FR13 Stage 3).
* **Impact:** The matching engine (FR10) expects a 12-category flat macro JSON. FR13 builds a highly siloed, Neo4j single-tenant graph database. Giving both the same `DEP-ID` will cause catastrophic parse errors in the matching pipeline.
* **Required Resolution:** FR13 must mint a new DEP-ID for the 1:1 Client Neo4j Map (e.g., `DEP-ENG-030`), or FR10 must specifically clarify that it relies on a different export format.

## WARNINGS (To monitor during development)

* **Risk:** FR19 activates at the very end of compilation (Gate C-06). If it detects an Escape Mode piece with High Semantic Affinity, it halts and offers the Operator the option to "Reclassify as Processing Mode." 
* **Architecture Note:** Because it is at C-06, simply tagging it "Processing Mode" is dangerous. The **FR18 Psychological Routing Brief** executes *before* this. If the mode changes from Escape to Processing, the entire FR18 module needs to be re-run, because Processing Mode requires entirely different arousal, valence, and SDT variables. The dashboard must warn the operator that resolution requires a pipeline rewind, not just a tag swap.

* **Architecture Note:** These must be formally admitted to the schema repository to ensure downstream parsers understand their nested structures.
