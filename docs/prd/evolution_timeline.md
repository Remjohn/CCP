**Purpose:** This document is the **mandatory filter** for all PRD module writing. Every concept referenced in a PRD module must be checked against this timeline to ensure it reflects the **current** architectural truth, not a superseded position.

## Retained Research Lineage Rule

When reading older documentation, use this rule:

1. **Keep the mechanism if it still improves truth, safety, control, or leverage.**
2. **Replace the framing if the commercial story or product center changed.**
3. **Reject only the parts that are explicitly superseded by Era 3.**

This means older docs are still valid when they define:

- retained dependencies
- guards and validators
- orchestration contracts
- intelligence collection methods
- visual or sonic control laws
- context reasoning and memory layers

They are **not** current truth when they define:

- synchronous-first product centers
- tripwire-first commercial logic
- JV-led distribution as the default growth engine
- live roleplay as the main skill surface

## The Invisible App Doctrine (Era 3 — Architectural Constant)

CCP has **exactly two client touchpoints**. Nothing else.

| Touchpoint | What the Client Experiences | What Runs Behind |
|---|---|---|
| **AFFiNE Dashboard** | Clean coach workspace — client cards, progress rings, streak flames, content delivery, red flag feed | CRAL, primitive orchestration, coalition formation, tenant isolation, PostgreSQL, Redis |
| **Telegram** | Voice notes coaching, reactions, challenges, content drops, accountability, payments | DSPy pipelines, Pydantic validation, biometric scoring, NIM inference, Stripe webhooks |

There is no separate web portal, no mobile app, no content studio interface, no media factory dashboard. The backend sophistication (Skia rendering, SCRE research, CMF pipeline, Voice DNA retuning, coalition benchmarking) is **invisible**. The user never sees it. They see simple, engaging, over-delivered experiences.

### The 4 Voice Notes Coaching Programs ARE the Product

The entire product surface is **voice notes coaching sessions** across 4 programs:

1. **Law28 Public Speaking** — conviction, clarity, pause architecture, anti-hedging, authority under constraint
2. **Webinar Sales Delivery** — structure, persuasion, pacing, CTA pressure, teaching-while-selling
3. **Networking Conversations Mastery (OFAP)** — opening lines, social context, warmth, follow-up, offline confidence
4. **Social Co-Creations Charisma (Conscious Reactions)** — reaction energy, charm, contrast, humor, co-created authority

### The Self-Translation Principle

Every coaching session simultaneously produces two kinds of value without the coach doing extra work:

1. **Content Assets** — shorts, carousels, memes, quote visuals, benchmark scorecards. These are the **exhaust** of practice, not a separate production workflow.
2. **Brand DNA/RNA Refinement** — every recording enriches the Voice DNA (Core/Style/Growth layers), feeds the CRAL evidence field, and strengthens the primitive coalition history.

The coach practices → the system produces. There is no separate "content creation" step. There is no "open the media factory and configure your output." The content appears because the coach showed up and spoke.

## WHAT WENT UP (Elevated in Era 3 — Current Truth)

### ⬆️ Conscious Reactions → Core Acquisition Engine
- **Before (Era 1-2):** Not mentioned. Trivianar was the acquisition/engagement surface.
- **Now (Era 3):** `Conscious Reactions` is the **default async branded engine** and the primary acquisition event. 1,384-line Source of Truth. Four modes: Solo, Debate, Jury, Tier List. Replaces Trivianar as the daily engagement layer.
- **Source:** `Conscious_Reactions_Source_of_Truth.md` (§1, §5.6.9), `Pricing_Silent_Referral_CoCreation_Architecture.md` (§7)

### ⬆️ Async-First Communication Skill Ladder → The New Center
- **Before (Era 1-2):** Live roleplay was the flagship skill surface. Synchronous practice was the center.
- **Now (Era 3):** Four async-first skill surfaces replace roleplay as the core: **(1) Law28 Public Speaking, (2) Webinar Sales Delivery, (3) Networking Conversations Mastery for OFAP, (4) Social Co-Creations Charisma with Conscious Reactions.** Daily async repetition is the moat, not live coordination.
- **Source:** `Communication_Skill_Ladder_Architecture.md` (§1-§4, §13)

### ⬆️ $29/$99 Value Ladder → Replaces Tripwire Pricing
- **Before (Era 2):** $16.95 tripwire → $39.95/week → $49.95/week funnel (from CBAR Pricing Experiment)
- **Now (Era 3):** Free Proof Layer → 7-Day Trial → $29/mo Continuity → $99/mo Full Package. The tripwire-first model is explicitly rejected. Speed of first delivery and Conscious Reactions are the conversion engines.
- **Source:** `Pricing_Silent_Referral_CoCreation_Architecture.md` (§2-§5)

### ⬆️ Silent Referral Architecture → Replaces Explicit Referral Programs
- **Before (Era 2):** Advocate Ledger (DeSo-style status economy) scored 133/200 as a referral tool
- **Now (Era 3):** Silent Referral through reaction sharing, vote recruitment, co-created content loops. Referral is a **side effect of participation**, not an ask. "The strongest growth loop is not 'please refer us' — it is the reaction loop that silently onboards the next person."
- **Source:** `Pricing_Silent_Referral_CoCreation_Architecture.md` (§3, §6)

### ⬆️ Meaning/Experience Plane Separation → Formally Architected
- **Before (Era 1-2):** Implicit. Primitives were vaguely "content atoms."
- **Now (Era 3):** Two distinct planes with separate schemas, separate registries, separate validation standards. 10 Meaning families + 8 Experience families. 150+ codified YAML primitives.
- **Source:** `Primitive_Family_Classification_CCP_CMF.md`, `Perceptual_Primitives_Architecture.md`

### ⬆️ Coalition Theory → Replaces Single-Primitive Selection
- **Before (Era 1-2):** Content was generated from single archetype/angle selection.
- **Now (Era 3):** Weighted coalition signatures formed from surviving primitive candidates. Coalition benchmarking. Anti-Centroid Law enforcement.
- **Source:** `Perceptual_Primitives_Architecture.md` (§7-§10)

### ⬆️ Voice DNA 3-Layer Growth Model → Replaces Static Voice Extraction
- **Before (Era 1-2):** Voice DNA was a static extraction fingerprint.
- **Now (Era 3):** Three layers: Core (invariant identity), Style State (current season), Growth Delta (emerging strengths). Non-Imitation Doctrine. Periodic retuning.
- **Source:** `Voice_DNA_Growth_Doctrine.md`

### ⬆️ Sovereign GPU / Self-Hosted NIM → Replaces Third-Party API Dependency

2. **NEVER reference the $16.95/$39.95/$49.95 pricing.** The current pricing is $0 → $29/mo → $99/mo.
3. **NEVER center live roleplay.** It is de-centered. The center is the async Skill Ladder.
4. **NEVER reference calendar-gated progression.** It is biometric-gated.
5. **ALWAYS distinguish Meaning Plane from Experience Plane** when discussing primitives.
6. **ALWAYS reference the 4 Skill Surfaces** (Law28, Webinar, Networking OFAP, Social Co-Creations) as the current architecture, not roleplay.
7. **Advocate Ledger, Whale Slider, Crucible Filter** are backlogged concepts — reference only as deferred/future if at all.
8. **When citing MCDA features**, always note their Era 3 status (Active, Absorbed, Deferred, Retained).
9. **The product has EXACTLY two client touchpoints**: AFFiNE Dashboard + Telegram. Never reference separate web portals, mobile apps, content studio interfaces, or media factory dashboards.
10. **Every coaching session self-translates** into content assets and Brand DNA/RNA refinement. Never describe content creation as a separate workflow the coach must initiate.
11. **NEVER reference JV, Joint Venture, or partner-driven distribution.** Growth is through Silent Referral (participation-driven). Founding Partners are early adopters, not distributors.
12. **Do not discard an older mechanism only because it predates Era 3.** If March or early-April research introduced a still-valid guard, dependency, or intelligence layer, preserve the mechanism and update the framing.
13. **The following older mechanisms remain active unless explicitly replaced:** Psychological Routing, Semantic Affinity Guard, Audience Maturity, Client Intelligence Layer, Cultural Memory Map, Coach Story Archive, Context Performance Registry, Context Reasoning, and first-frame visual control.
